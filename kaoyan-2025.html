Text 1（莎士比亚教学研究）

The grammar school boy from Stratford-upon-Avon has landed a scholarly punch after groundbreaking research showed that Shakespeare does benefit children’s literacy and emotional development. But only if you act him out.

A study found that a “rehearsal room” approach to teaching Shakespeare broadened children’s vocabulary and the complexity of their writing as well as their emotional literacy. “The research shows that the way actors work makes a big difference to the way children use language and also how they think about themselves,” Jacqui O’Hanlon of the Royal Shakespeare Company (RSC), which commissioned the study, said.

The randomised control trial involved hundreds of year 5 pupils — aged nine and ten — at 45 state primary schools that had not been “previously exposed to RSC pedagogy.” They were split into target and control groups and asked to write, for example, a message in a bottle as Ferdinand following the shipwreck in The Tempest. The target group were given a 30-minute drama-based activity to accompany the passage.

The peer-reviewed results showed that the target group of pupils drew on a wider vocabulary, used words “classed as more sophisticated or rarer”, and wrote at greater length. They also “appear to be more comfortable writing in role…while [control] pupils imagine how they themselves would react to being shipwrecked, [target] children put themselves in the shoes of a literary character and express that character’s emotion”. The Time to Act study also found that while control pupils relied on “desert island clichés” such as palm trees, target pupils were “more expansive [giving] a broader picture of the sky, the sea and the atmospheric conditions”.

O’Hanlon said she had been most surprised by the “emotional literacy that was evident in the [target] children’s writing” and that they were “more resilient in their writing, more hopeful”. The study showed the importance of embedding arts in education, she said.

But could the results be replicated with any old dramatist? O’Hanlon said more research would be needed but suggested that Shakespeare’s use of 20,000 words, compared with the everyday 2,000 words, gave a “massive expansion of language into children’s lives”.

长难句精讲（四步拆句 · 一目了然）

四步法：① 找主干（几个谓语切几段，先划掉从句/插入语）→ ② 理枝叶（每块修饰谁）→ ③ 排语序（定语前置/长状语拆句）→ ④ 出译文。

1. This area of the Aegean Sea is prone to earthquakes and tsunamis, which caused the city to gradually sink.

① 主干This area is prone to earthquakes and tsunamis.（该地区易发生地震与海啸。）
② 枝叶which… → 非限制性定语从句，修饰前面整件事（地震海啸）；cause sb/sth to do。
③ 语序定语从句译“这（些灾害）使该城逐渐下沉”。
④ 译文爱琴海这一地区易发生地震与海啸，这使该城逐渐下沉。

2. In recent decades, shifting currents and climate change have eroded a natural barrier that protected Pavlopetri.

① 主干Shifting currents and climate change have eroded a barrier.（洋流与气候变化侵蚀了屏障。）
② 枝叶that protected Pavlopetri → 定语从句，修饰 barrier。
③ 语序定语从句前置：“保护帕夫洛佩特里的天然屏障”。
④ 译文近几十年来，变化的洋流与气候变化侵蚀了保护帕夫洛佩特里的天然屏障。

3. The carbon emissions from technological research are well spent: ultimately this research will safeguard the future of our planet.

① 主干The carbon emissions are well spent.（这些碳排放是值得的。）
② 枝叶冒号后为解释说明（ultimately…）；be well spent 被动表“花得值”。
③ 语序冒号译“：”，后句顺译。
④ 译文技术研究产生的碳排放是值得的：归根结底，这些研究将守护地球的未来。

4. But demonstrating the efficacy of science will be crucial if we are to solve humanity’s greatest challenges.

① 主干Demonstrating the efficacy of science will be crucial.（证明科学的有效性至关重要。）
② 枝叶demonstrating… → 动名词作主语；if we are to do → 条件状语（be to do 表“要……的话”）。
③ 语序条件从句提前译“若要解决……”。
④ 译文但若要解决人类最大的挑战，证明科学的有效性将至关重要。

5. A great example of this is Europe’s largest supercomputer, LUMI in Finland, which is astonishingly carbon-negative.

① 主干A great example is LUMI.（一个很好的例子是 LUMI。）
② 枝叶Europe’s largest supercomputer → 同位语；which… → 非限制性定语从句，补充说明 LUMI。
③ 语序同位语与定语从句均作补充，可后置顺译。
④ 译文一个很好的例子是欧洲最大的超级计算机——芬兰的 LUMI，它惊人地实现了负碳排放。

6. Even when a digital object is preserved, it may only be the carrier that’s saved, not the information itself.

① 主干It may only be the carrier that’s saved.（被保存的可能只是载体。）
② 枝叶Even when… → 让步状语；it is… that… → 强调句，强调 the carrier。
③ 语序让步先译“即使……”；强调句译“只是……被保存”。
④ 译文即使数字对象被保存下来，被保存的也可能只是载体，而非信息本身。

7. While there is some optimism that emerging countermeasures may see piracy begin to plateau by 2027, there is no consensus among stakeholders as to when it may begin to decline. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干There is no consensus as to when it may decline.（对于何时下降并无共识。）
② 枝叶While… → 让步状语；that… → 同位语从句说明 optimism；as to when… → 介词后接宾语从句。
③ 语序While 让步先译“尽管……”；主句“但……尚无共识”。
④ 译文尽管有人乐观地认为新的对策可能使盗版在 2027 年前趋于平稳，但利益相关方对盗版何时开始下降仍无共识。

8. Scientists have employed a variety of ways to engage the general public in their research, such as making data analysis into an online game or sample collection into a smartphone application.

① 主干Scientists have employed a variety of ways.（科学家采用了多种方式。）
② 枝叶to engage… → 目的/定语；such as… → 举例；making A into B 与 (making) sample collection into… 并列。
③ 语序such as 列举顺译，两个 make…into… 并列处理。
④ 译文科学家采用了多种方式让公众参与研究，例如把数据分析做成在线游戏，或把样本采集做成手机应用。

语法专项（本卷句子 · 先做后对）

本套卷语法考点清单与教学

一、时态：一般现在 / 现在完成 / 一般过去

客观事实用一般现在（This area is prone to…）；由过去持续至今用现在完成（have eroded / have employed）；叙述历史事件用一般过去（caused / formed）。

二、被动语态

动作承受者作主语用 be+过去分词：are well spent / is preserved / be saved；注意与“系表结构”区分。

三、非谓语动词

动名词作主语（demonstrating the efficacy of science）；不定式表目的/定语（ways to engage…）；分词作定语（shifting currents / emerging countermeasures）。

四、三大从句

定语从句：which caused… / that protected…；让步状语从句：Even when… / While…；同位语从句：optimism that…；宾语从句：as to when it may decline。
