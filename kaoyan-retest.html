一、自我介绍模板（约 1–1.5 分钟）

替换 ___ 处填自己的信息；结构：问候 → 基本信息 → 学业/兴趣 → 经历 → 报考理由 → 收尾。

Good morning / afternoon, dear professors. It's a great honor to be here for this interview.

My name is ___ (name), and I come from ___ (city), ___ (province). I am a senior student at ___ University, majoring in ___ (major).

During my undergraduate years, I have developed a strong interest in ___. I have taken courses such as ___ and ___; my GPA is ___, and I ranked ___ in my class.

I also joined ___(project / competition / research) and learned how to ___. Last year, I did an internship at ___, which taught me ___.

In my spare time, I enjoy ___ and ___, which help me relax and keep energetic.

The reason why I apply for ___ at your university is that ___(academic strength / faculty / lab / my career goal).

If I am lucky enough to be admitted, I will work hard, read widely and try to make some progress in ___. Thank you for your time.

二、复试常见问答（含英文参考回答）

个人信息 & 背景

Q Tell me about yourself.

I am a senior student at ___ University, majoring in ___.

During my college years, I have acquired solid basic knowledge and developed a keen interest in ___.

I also took part in ___ and completed an internship at ___, which improved my practical ability.

I'm a diligent, patient and cooperative person, and I believe these qualities will help me in postgraduate study.

中文要点：3–5 句讲清：基本信息→专业兴趣→经历/能力→性格收尾。

Q What are your strengths and weaknesses?

I think my greatest strength is persistence. Once I set a goal, I will keep working on it until I succeed.

I'm also a good team player and enjoy cooperating with others.

As for my weakness, sometimes I pay too much attention to details and spend too much time on small things.

But I am trying to improve it by setting clear priorities and managing my time better.

中文要点：优点用例子支撑；缺点选可改进、非致命的，并给改进措施。

Q Where are you from? / Tell me about your hometown.

I come from ___, a city in ___ Province.

It is famous for ___ and has a long history.

People there are hard-working and friendly, which has influenced me a lot.

中文要点：地理+特色+对人的影响，1–3 句即可。

Q Tell me about your university.

I study at ___ University, which is well known for ___.

It has a beautiful campus and excellent teachers.

Studying there has broadened my horizons and helped me grow into a more independent person.

中文要点：学校特色+带给你的成长。

Q What do you do in your spare time?

In my spare time, I like reading and jogging.

Reading helps me gain knowledge, while jogging helps me keep fit and relieve pressure.

Sometimes I also watch English movies to improve my listening and speaking.

中文要点：爱好+益处（可顺带提英语学习）。

学业 & 专业

Q Why did you choose your major?

I chose ___ mainly because I am genuinely interested in it.

When I took the course ___, I was deeply attracted by ___, and I decided to learn more about it.

Besides, this major has broad prospects and can help me realize my career goal.

中文要点：兴趣来源（具体课程/经历）+ 前景/目标。

Q What is your favorite course and why?

My favorite course is ___, taught by Professor ___.

It not only gave me systematic knowledge but also trained my ability to think logically.

The teacher often encouraged us to discuss and ask questions, which made the class lively and inspiring.

中文要点：课程+收获+教学方式。

Q What is your graduation thesis about?

My graduation thesis focuses on ___.

In the thesis, I mainly discuss ___ and try to ___.

Through writing it, I learned how to find literature, analyze data and organize my ideas logically.

中文要点：题目+主要内容/方法+收获（数据/文献能力）。

Q How did you prepare for the postgraduate entrance exam?

I made a detailed plan and stuck to it.

I spent about ___ hours a day on English and professional courses, and reviewed my mistakes every week.

Although it was hard, the process made me more self-disciplined and persistent.

中文要点：计划+执行+坚持的收获。

科研 & 实践

Q Do you have any research experience?

Yes. I joined a research project on ___ under the guidance of Professor ___.

I was responsible for ___(collecting data / doing experiments / reviewing literature).

This experience taught me how to do research step by step and how to cooperate with a team.

中文要点：项目+你的角色+能力/收获。没有也可说课程设计/毕业论文。

Q Have you done any internship? What did you learn?

Yes. I did an internship at ___ last ___.

My main task was ___.

From it, I learned how to apply knowledge to practice and how to communicate with others in a workplace.

中文要点：单位/岗位+任务+收获。

Q Tell me about a time you worked in a team.

In a group project, we had to finish ___ within a short time.

I was in charge of ___, and I kept communicating with my teammates to make sure we were on the same page.

Finally, we completed the task on time, and I realized that communication and cooperation are the key to teamwork.

中文要点：情境+任务+行动+结果（STAR 结构）。

Q Describe a challenge you faced and how you solved it.

Once I had difficulty in ___(a course / a project).

At first I felt frustrated, but I decided to face it instead of giving up.

I broke the problem into small parts, asked my teacher for advice, and practiced again and again.

In the end, I ___, and I learned that persistence and a positive attitude really matter.

中文要点：问题+态度+行动+结果+感悟。

报考动机 & 规划

Q Why do you choose our university?

Your university has an excellent reputation in ___, and I really admire its academic atmosphere.

I have read some papers by Professor ___, whose research on ___ interests me a lot.

I believe studying here will give me a better platform and help me achieve my goal.

中文要点：学校/专业优势+具体导师或方向+对你的意义。

Q Why do you want to pursue a master's degree?

First, I want to deepen my understanding of ___ instead of staying at the surface.

Second, I hope to improve my research ability through systematic training.

Third, a master's degree will prepare me better for my future career.

中文要点：First/Second/Third 分点，动机+能力+职业。

Q What is your study plan for the next three years?

In the first year, I will focus on taking courses and reading as much literature as possible.

In the second year, I plan to take part in research projects and try to publish a paper.

In the third year, I will finish my graduation thesis and prepare for my future career.

中文要点：按年分阶段：课程→科研→论文/就业。

Q What are your career plans after graduation?

I hope to work in ___(field), such as a university, a research institute or a company.

If possible, I would like to continue doing research related to ___.

Anyway, I will try my best to turn what I have learned into real value.

中文要点：方向+理由+态度。

兴趣、生活 & 开放题

Q What kind of books do you like?

I like reading books on ___ and ___, such as ___.

They not only broaden my horizons but also help me think more deeply.

中文要点：类型+具体书名+收获。

Q How do you handle stress or pressure?

When I feel stressed, I usually ___ (listen to music / do sports / talk with friends).

I also make a to-do list to deal with problems one by one.

I think a positive attitude and a clear plan are the best ways to reduce pressure.

中文要点：方法+态度。

Q Who is the person you admire most?

The person I admire most is ___(my father / a scientist / a writer).

He/She is ___, and his/her ___ spirit has influenced me a lot.

I hope I can be a person like him/her — hard-working and never giving up.

中文要点：人物+品质+对你的影响。

Q What is your opinion on ___ (a hot issue)?

I think this is an important issue that deserves our attention.

On the one hand, ___; on the other hand, ___.

In my opinion, we should ___ in order to make things better.

中文要点：万能框架：表态→两面→建议；不熟悉也别沉默。

Q Do you think English is important? Why?

Yes, I think English is very important.

As a tool, it helps me read international papers and communicate with people from other countries.

For a postgraduate student, good English is also the basis of doing good research.

中文要点：观点+两个理由+与读研的关系。

三、听不懂/紧张时的救场句

  • Pardon? / I beg your pardon? （没听清，请重复）
  • Could you please repeat the question? / Would you mind saying that again?
  • I'm sorry, I didn't quite catch that. Could you speak a little slower?
  • That's a good question. Let me think for a moment.
  • Well, that's an interesting question. I'd like to answer it from two aspects.
  • May I rephrase my answer? / Let me put it in another way.
  • I'm not sure about that, but I would guess / I would like to say that…
  • Sorry, I'm not familiar with that. Could I talk about ___ instead?
  • That's all I can think of for now. Thank you.

四、常用连接词与高级句型

  • 开头：Thank you for your question. / Well, let me start by saying…
  • 分点：First of all… Besides… What's more… Finally… / On the one hand… on the other hand…
  • 举例：For example… / Take ___ as an example… / such as…
  • 因果：because / since / as a result / therefore / that's why…
  • 让步：Although…, I still… / In spite of…, …
  • 总结：In a word… / All in all… / To sum up… / That's why I…
  • 观点：In my opinion… / From my point of view… / As far as I'm concerned…
  • 结尾：That's all. Thank you for listening. / Thanks for your time.

五、面试礼仪与注意事项

要点做法
礼貌开场与结束进门问好（Good morning, professors.）；结束时道谢（Thank you for your time.）。
听清再答没听清就用救场句请老师重复，切勿沉默或乱答；紧张时可深呼吸、放慢语速。
总分结构回答用 First / Second / Finally，条理清晰比词汇高级更重要。
扬长避短不会的题顺着话题转移到你熟悉的领域，别硬编事实。
控制时间每问 30–60 秒；自我介绍控制 1–1.5 分钟。
着装与礼仪整洁大方、坐姿端正、与老师有眼神交流、面带微笑。
诚实不懂就说不懂，并表示愿意学习，态度比答案更打动人。

六、练习建议

① 先**读熟**自我介绍模板，改成自己的版本并背下；② 每个问题**先说 30–60 秒**，用手机录音，再对照范文找差距；③ 重点背「救场句」和连接词；④ 每天练 3–5 题，一周覆盖全部；⑤ 找同学/老师模拟面试 1–2 次。
