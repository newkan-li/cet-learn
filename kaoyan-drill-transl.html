2020 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. With the Church’s teachings and ways of thinking being eclipsed by the Renaissance, the gap between the Medieval and modern periods had been bridged, leading to new and unexplored intellectual territories.

随着文艺复兴使教会的教义和思维方式黯然失色，中世纪与现代之间的鸿沟得以弥合，从而开启了崭新的、未被探索的知识领域。

技巧：with + 名词 + being done 独立结构表伴随/原因；eclipse 使失色；bridge the gap 弥合鸿沟；leading to 分词作结果。

47. Before each of their revelations, many thinkers at the time had sustained more ancient ways of thinking, including the geocentric view that the Earth was at the centre of our universe.

在他们各自作出这些揭示之前，当时的许多思想家一直固守着更古老的思维方式，包括认为地球位于宇宙中心的地心说。

技巧：revelation 揭示；sustain 维持/固守；including… 补充；that 从句作同位语修饰 the geocentric view。

48. Despite attempts by the Church to suppress this new generation of logicians and rationalists, more explanations for how the universe functioned were being made at a rate that the people could no longer ignore.

尽管教会试图压制这一代新的逻辑学家和理性主义者，但关于宇宙如何运行的解释层出不穷，其速度之快已让民众无法再忽视。

技巧：Despite + 名词短语表让步；suppress 压制；how the universe functioned 宾语从句；at a rate that… 定语从句修饰 rate。

49. As many took on the duty of trying to integrate reasoning and scientific philosophies into the world, the Renaissance was over and it was time for a new era — the Age of Reason.

随着许多人承担起把理性与科学哲学融入世界的责任，文艺复兴结束了，一个新的时代——理性时代——到来了。

技巧：as 引导时间/原因；take on the duty 承担责任；integrate A into B 把 A 融入 B；it was time for… 是……的时候了。

50. Such actions to seek knowledge and to understand what information we already knew were captured by the Latin phrase “sapere aude” or “dare to know”, after Immanuel Kant used it in his essay “An Answer to the Question: What is Enlightenment?”.

这种探求知识、理解已知信息的行动，被拉丁语短语“sapere aude”（即“敢于求知”）所概括——伊曼努尔·康德在其《答复这个问题：什么是启蒙？》一文中使用了这一短语。

技巧：Such actions… were captured by… 被动；what information we already knew 宾语从句；after 引导时间状语。

2021 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. Those societies came out of the war with levels of enrollment that had been roughly constant at 3%-5% of the relevant age groups during the decades before the war.

这些社会在战后初期的高等教育入学率，与战前数十年间大致持平，约占相关年龄组的3%—5%。

技巧：come out of the war 战后初期；with + 名词短语表伴随；that 定语从句修饰 levels of enrollment。

47. And the demand that rose in those societies for entry to higher education extended to groups and social classes that had not thought of attending a university before the war.

这些社会中兴起的接受高等教育的需求，扩展到了战前从未想过上大学的群体和社会阶层。

技巧：the demand for entry to… 对入学的需求；that rose… 定语从句；extend to 扩展到；that had not thought of… 定语从句。

48. in many countries of Western Europe, the numbers of students in higher education doubled within five-year periods during the 1960s and doubled again in seven, eight, or 10 years by the middle of the 1970s.

在西欧许多国家，20 世纪 60 年代高等教育的学生人数每五年翻一番，到 70 年代中期又在七到十年内再次翻番。

技巧：doubled within… periods 每五年翻番；by the middle of the 1970s 到 70 年代中期；注意两个 doubled 的并列。

49. and when the new staff are predominantly young men and women fresh from postgraduate study, they largely define the norms of academic life in that faculty.

而当新进教师大多是刚毕业的研究生（年轻男女）时，他们很大程度上界定了该院系学术生活的规范。

技巧：when 引导时间状语从句；predominantly 主要是；fresh from 刚从……毕业；define the norms 界定规范。

50. High growth rates increased the chances for academic innovation; they also weakened the forms and processes by which teachers and students are admitted into a community of scholars during periods of stability or slow growth.

高增长率增加了学术创新的机会；但也削弱了在稳定或缓慢增长时期教师和学生被接纳进学者共同体的形式与程序。

技巧：分号并列；by which… 介词+which 引导定语从句修饰 forms and processes；be admitted into 被接纳进。

2022 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. It was also, and this is unknown even to many people well read about the period, a battle between those who made codes and those who broke them.

这同时也是一场制定密码者与破译密码者之间的较量——这一点甚至连许多对该时期颇有研究的人都不知道。

技巧：插入语 and this is unknown… 用破折号译出；those who made codes and those who broke them 两个定语从句；make/break codes 编制/破译密码。

47. It listed many documents in code that had been captured from the French army of Spain, and whose secrets had been revealed by the work of one George Scovell, an officer in British headquarters.

它列出了许多从西班牙的法军那里缴获的密码文件，这些文件的秘密已被一位名叫乔治·斯科维尔的英国司令部军官破解。

技巧：documents in code 密码文件；that had been captured… 与 whose secrets… 并列的定语从句；an officer… 作同位语。

48. he could not analyze carefully what this obscure officer may or may not have contributed to that great struggle between nations or indeed tell us anything much about the man himself.

他无法仔细分析这位默默无闻的军官对那场国家间的伟大斗争可能有过（或没有过）什么贡献，也难以告诉我们关于此人本身的更多情况。

技巧：what 引导宾语从句；may or may not have contributed 表“是否曾有贡献”；or indeed 进一步并列谓语 tell us…。

49. There may have been many spies and intelligence officers during the Napoleonic Wars, but it is usually extremely difficult to find the material they actually provided or worked on.

拿破仑战争期间或许有许多间谍和情报官员，但通常极难找到他们实际提供或处理的材料。

技巧：There may have been 表推测“或许曾有”；but 转折；they actually provided or worked on 为省略关系词的定语从句。

50. Just as the code breaking has its wider relevance in the struggle for Spain, so his attempts to make his way up the promotion ladder speak volumes about British society.

正如密码破译在争夺西班牙的斗争中具有更广泛的意义一样，他努力在晋升阶梯上向上攀登的经历，也充分说明了英国社会的情况。

技巧：Just as…, so… 引导方式/类比结构；make one’s way up the promotion ladder 在晋升阶梯上向上爬；speak volumes about 充分说明。

2023 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. AI can also be used to identify the lifestyle choices of customers regarding their hobbies, favourite celebrities, music choices, and fashions to provide unique content in marketing messages put out through social media.

人工智能还可用于识别客户在爱好、喜爱的名人、音乐选择和时尚方面的生活方式选择，从而为通过社交媒体发布的营销信息提供独特的内容。

技巧：be used to do 被用来做；identify the lifestyle choices 识别生活方式选择；regarding… 关于；to provide… 表目的；put out through social media 过去分词作后置定语。

47. Some believe that AI is negatively impacting on the marketer’s role by reducing creativity and removing jobs, but they are aware that it is a way of reducing costs and creating new information.

一些人认为，人工智能通过削弱创造力和减少工作岗位对营销人员的角色产生了负面影响，但他们也意识到，这是降低成本、创造新信息的一种方式。

技巧：that… 宾语从句；by reducing… 表方式；but 转折；be aware that… 意识到。

48. Algorithms that are used to simulate human interactions are creating many of these concerns, especially as no-one is quite sure what the outcomes of using AI to interact with customers will be.

用于模拟人类互动的算法正引发许多此类担忧，尤其是因为没有人十分确定用人工智能与客户互动会产生什么结果。

技巧：that… 定语从句修饰 algorithms；especially as… 表原因；what 引导宾语从句作 sure 的宾语。

49. If customers are not willing to share data, AI will be starved of essential information and will not be able to function effectively or employ machine learning to improve its marketing content and communication.

如果客户不愿分享数据，人工智能就会缺乏必要的信息，无法有效运作，也无法运用机器学习来改进其营销内容和沟通。

技巧：If… 条件状语从句；be starved of 缺乏；两个并列谓语 will not be able to function… or employ…。

50. The non-intrusive delivery of the marketing messages in a way that is sensitive to the needs of the target customer is one of the critical challenges to the digital marketer.

以敏锐体察目标客户需求的方式非侵入性地传递营销信息，是数字营销者面临的重大挑战之一。

技巧：主语为 The non-intrusive delivery；in a way that… 方式状语从句；be sensitive to 体察/顾及；one of the critical challenges 之一。

2024 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. They sometimes travel more than sixty miles to find food or water, and are very good at working out where other elephants are — even when they are out of sight.

它们有时要跋涉六十多英里去寻找食物或水，而且非常善于判断其他大象的位置——即使对方在视线之外。

技巧：travel… to find 表目的；be good at doing 擅长；where… 宾语从句；even when… 让步状语从句。

47. The researchers are convinced that the elephants always know precisely where they are in relation to all the resources they need, and can therefore take shortcuts, as well as following familiar routes.

研究人员确信，大象总是精确地知道自己在与所需各种资源相对的位置，因此既能走熟悉的路线，也能抄近路。

技巧：be convinced that… 确信；where… 宾语从句；in relation to 相对于；take shortcuts 抄近路；as well as following… 也走……。

48. One possibility was that they merely used their eyes and tried out the plants they found, but that would probably result in a lot of wasted time and energy, not least because their eyesight is actually not very good.

一种可能是，它们仅仅用眼睛观察并尝试所发现的植物，但这很可能造成大量时间和精力的浪费，尤其是因为它们的视力其实并不好。

技巧：that… 表语从句；they found 省略关系词的定语从句；result in 导致；not least because… 尤其是因为。

49. The volatile chemicals produced by plants can be carried a long way, and they are very characteristic: Each plant or tree has its own particular odor signature.

植物产生的挥发性化学物质可以被带到很远的地方，而且极具特征：每种植物或树木都有自己独特的气味特征。

技巧：produced by plants 过去分词作后置定语；be carried 被动；冒号后为解释说明。

50. The experiment showed that elephants may well use smell to identify patches of trees that are good to eat, and secondly to assess the quality of the trees within each patch.

该实验表明，大象很可能利用气味来识别适合食用的树丛，其次还用它来评估每一片树丛中树木的质量。

技巧：that… 宾语从句；may well 很可能；use smell to identify… and to assess… 两个并列目的；that are good to eat 定语从句。

2025 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. Recent decades have seen science move into a convention where engagement in the subject can only be done through institutions, such as a university.

近几十年来，科学已形成一种惯例：要参与科学（研究），只能通过大学等机构来进行。

技巧：see 表“见证/发生”；convention 惯例；where 从句修饰 convention；engagement in the subject 参与该学科。

47. But by utilising the natural curiosity of the general public it is possible to overcome many of these challenges by engaging non-scientists directly in the research process.

但是，利用公众天生的好奇心，让非科学家直接参与研究过程，就有可能克服其中许多挑战。

技巧：by doing 表方式；it is possible to… 译“就有可能……”；engage sb in 使参与。

48. Scientists have employed a variety of ways to engage the general public in their research, such as making data analysis into an online game or sample collection into a smartphone application.

科学家们采用了多种方式让公众参与研究，例如把数据分析做成在线游戏，或把样本采集做成手机应用。

技巧：a variety of 多种；such as 举例；make A into B 把 A 变成 B（两个并列）。

49. These groups of people are part of a rapidly expanding biotechnological social movement of citizen scientists and professional scientists seeking to take discovery out of institutions and put it into the hands of anyone with the enthusiasm.

这些人是迅速壮大的生物技术社会运动的一部分，成员包括公民科学家与专业科学家，他们力图让科学发现走出机构，交到任何有热情的人手中。

技巧：seeking to 作定语修饰 scientists；take… out of… and put… into… 并列；anyone with the enthusiasm 有热情的人。

50. They pool resources, collaborate, think outside the box, and find solutions and ways around obstacles to explore science for the sake of science without the traditional boundaries of working inside a formal setting.

他们汇聚资源、开展合作、跳出固有思维，寻找解决方案与绕开障碍的办法，为科学而探索科学，不受在正式机构内工作的传统界限束缚。

技巧：pool resources 汇聚资源；think outside the box 跳出固有思维；for the sake of 为了；without… boundaries 不受……束缚。

2026 年 · 英译中（46–50）

46. Tracing the history of the term, we can see how the definition of scientific literacy has shifted over time, muddying the waters when it comes to determining the goals of science education.

追溯这一术语的历史，我们可以看到“科学素养”的定义如何随时间而变化，这使得确定科学教育目标的问题变得更加模糊。

技巧：Tracing… 现在分词作状语；how… 宾语从句；muddying the waters 现在分词表结果“把水搅浑/使问题模糊”；when it comes to 涉及/谈到。

47. A return to that version of scientific literacy, which focused more on teaching what science is and how it works and less on memorizing scientific facts, seems like something society today desperately needs.

回归那种版本的“科学素养”——它更注重教授科学是什么、如何运作，而较少强调记忆科学事实——似乎正是当今社会迫切需要的。

技巧：which… 非限制性定语从句修饰 version；what science is / how it works 两个宾语从句；more…less… 比较；something (that) society needs 定语从句。

48. Educators advanced the idea of having students complete detailed laboratory exercises in high schools in the belief that such work was beneficial primarily as a way to enhance logical reasoning and observational skills.

教育工作者提出了让学生在高中完成详细实验练习的主张，他们相信这类工作主要作为增强逻辑推理和观察能力的方式而有益。

技巧：have sb do 使某人做；in the belief that… 相信……；primarily as a way to 主要作为……的方式。

49. It wasn’t until the phrase “scientific literacy” came along in the 1940s that science had the formidable slogan it needed to command public attention and make improving science education an important national goal.

直到 20 世纪 40 年代“科学素养”这一说法出现，科学才拥有了它所需要的有力口号，以赢得公众关注并使改进科学教育成为一项重要的国家目标。

技巧：It wasn’t until… that… 强调句“直到……才……”；it needed 省略关系词的定语从句修饰 slogan；command attention 赢得关注；make…an important national goal。

50. The intense focus on scientific literacy in the United States originally grew out of the critical role of science and technology during World War II, as well as the perceived deficiencies of American soldiers.

美国对“科学素养”的高度关注最初源于二战期间科学技术的关键作用，以及人们所认为的美国士兵的不足。

技巧：grow out of 源于；the critical role of… 关键作用；as well as 以及；perceived 过去分词作定语“所认为的”。
