2026年6月第2套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. Those people live in constant fear that their “deception” will be exposed.

译文：这些人时刻生活在担忧之中：自己的“欺骗”迟早会被揭穿。

结构拆解：

· 主谓结构：Those people live in constant fear（活在持续的恐惧中）。

· that 引导同位语从句，说明 fear 的具体内容。

· “deception” 带引号，表示“所谓的欺骗”。

翻译技巧：抽象名词（fear/idea/hope/news）+ that 从句多为同位语从句，译成“……的（恐惧）”。

2. They think that all of their successes are not a product of their skill or hard work, instead they attribute their own successes to external circumstances, for example, to luck and chance, or believe that their performance is massively overestimated by others.

译文：他们认为成功并非自身能力或努力的结果，反而把成功归因于外部环境（如运气与偶然），或者相信自己的表现被他人严重高估。

结构拆解：

· think 后接 that 宾语从句，主干 They think that… instead they… or believe that…，instead 表“相反、反而”。

· attribute A to B＝把 A 归因于 B。

· or believe that… 与前面 think 并列；overestimated by others 为被动语态。

翻译技巧：先找三个并列动作 think / attribute / believe，instead 译“反而”，长宾语从句逐层断开。

3. A permanent underestimation of one’s own abilities is often accompanied by the fear that this supposed intellectual deception will be exposed sooner or later and that they will pay the price for this.

译文：长期低估自身能力，往往伴随一种恐惧：担心这种所谓的智力欺骗迟早暴露，并为此付出代价。

结构拆解：

· 主句：A permanent underestimation… is accompanied by the fear（被……伴随）。

· fear 后两个并列 that 同位语从句：that… will be exposed and that they will pay the price。

· sooner or later 迟早；pay the price for 为……付出代价。

翻译技巧：the fear that… and that…：两个 that 从句都说明恐惧内容，并列处要看清。

4. It is astonishing how many cultures share this tool in approaching music.

译文：令人惊讶的是，有这么多文化在接触音乐时都使用同一个工具。

结构拆解：

· It 是形式主语，真正主语是 how many cultures share this tool 这个主语从句。

· in approaching music 表“在接触/处理音乐方面”。

翻译技巧：It is + adj + 主语从句 → 译“令人……的是，……”。

5. Using this five-note scale, musicians from around the globe have a medium through which they can relate, communicate, perform, and ultimately understand each other.

译文：借助这种五音音阶，全球的音乐家便拥有了一个媒介，通过它他们可以沟通、交流、演奏并最终理解彼此。

结构拆解：

· 现在分词 Using… 作方式状语，先译“借助……”。

· 主句：musicians… have a medium。

· through which 引导定语从句修饰 medium（还原为 through the medium），从句中并列四个谓语 relate/communicate/perform/understand。

翻译技巧：分词开头先译状语；through which 还原为“通过这个媒介”再顺译。

2026年6月第3套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. Children experience physical disgust from a very early age, but do not achieve an understanding of the concept until they are around four years old.

译文：儿童很小就体验到生理性厌恶，但要到约四岁才能真正理解这一概念。

结构拆解：

· but 连接两个并列谓语 experience / do not achieve。

· not…until… 直到……才……。

· around four years old 大约四岁。

翻译技巧：not…until… 译为“直到……才”，把否定落到主句。

2. Some say that when we refer to disgust in relation to moral issues, we are using disgust as a metaphor, and what we are feeling is in reality anger.

译文：有人说，当我们在道德问题上谈到“厌恶”时，其实是在把厌恶用作隐喻——我们真实的感受是愤怒。

结构拆解：

· say + that 宾语从句；从句内 when 引导时间状语。

· 主句部分为并列：we are using… and what we are feeling is…。

· what we are feeling 是主语从句，in reality 实为/其实。

翻译技巧：宾语从句较长时按“when 状语→主句→感觉对象”分层翻译。

3. Indeed, children whose parents talk about emotions often have a better emotional understanding than children whose parents do not discuss emotions often.

译文：事实上，父母常与他们谈论情绪的孩子，情绪理解力往往好于父母很少谈论情绪的孩子。

结构拆解：

· 主句：children… have a better emotional understanding than children…，比较结构。

· 两个 whose 分别引导定语从句修饰前后两个 children。

· 整句构成 A(whose…) 优于 B(whose…) 的对比。

翻译技巧：先译两处 whose“父母如何→孩子如何”，再套上 better…than 的比较。

4. Researchers Anuj Shah and Michael LaForest wondered whether our tendency to believe that social ties are reciprocal could lead us to mistakenly feel that a stranger does actually know us.

译文：研究者阿努杰·沙阿和迈克尔·拉福雷斯特想知道：我们“社会关系是相互的”这一倾向，会不会让我们误以为陌生人其实认识我们。

结构拆解：

· wondered + whether 宾语从句（是否……）。

· tendency to believe that… 不定式后接 that 从句修饰倾向。

· could lead us to feel that… 表“导致我们以为……”。

翻译技巧：whether 长宾语从句先抓链条：倾向→（导致）→误以为，再补细节。

5. The team found that residents of the developments that had received the leaflets believed it more likely that local officers would find out about illegal activity than residents of the control areas.

译文：团队发现，收到传单的住户比对照区的住户更相信当地警察会发现违法行为。

结构拆解：

· found + that 宾语从句（外层）。

· 从句主语 residents of the developments，后接 that had received 定语从句修饰 developments。

· believed it more likely that…：it 是形式宾语，真正的宾语是 that 从句；more…than… 构成比较。

翻译技巧：believed it + adj + that… 是形式宾语结构，等于 believed that… more likely，先译“更相信……”。

2025年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. Because how the body responds to a drug can differ depending on the time it is taken, it logically follows that taking medicines at specific times could help increase their effectiveness and reduce unwanted side effects.

译文：因为身体对药物的反应会随服药时间不同而不同，因此逻辑上，在特定时间服药能提高药效、减少副作用。

结构拆解：

· Because + 主语从句 how the body responds to a drug can differ……。

· the time it is taken 中 it is taken 是定语从句（省略 when）。

· 主句 it follows that…：it 形式主语，真正主语是 that 从句，其内 taking medicines 为动名词主语。

翻译技巧：两个 it 都是形式主语，分别还原成 how…与 taking… 再译。

2. Over 50 years ago, researchers found that the cholesterol drug simvastatin is more effective at lowering cholesterol levels when taken at night rather than during the day.

译文：五十多年前研究人员就发现，降胆固醇药辛伐他汀在夜间服用比白天服用更有效。

结构拆解：

· found + that 宾语从句。

· when taken at night 是省略主语的分词状语，还原为 when it is taken at night。

· rather than 表“而不是”，构成对比。

翻译技巧：when + 过去分词 → 还原成“当它被……时”，再译成“在夜间服用”。

3. In addition to people being healthier and living much longer, economists say that a combination of financial considerations such as years of slow rise in real wages and a shift away from traditional pensions in the private sector are some of the reasons people delay retirement.

译文：除了人们更健康、活得更久以外，经济学家指出，实际工资多年缓慢上涨、私营部门逐步退出传统养老金等一系列财务因素，正是人们推迟退休的部分原因。

结构拆解：

· In addition to + 动名词复合结构（people being healthier…），表“除了……之外”。

· say + that 宾语从句；从句主语是 a combination of financial considerations…，含 such as 举例列举。

· people delay retirement 是修饰 reasons 的定语从句（省略 why/for which）。

翻译技巧：长列举用 such as 断开，先译“诸如……等（因素）”，再落到 are the reasons。

4. Martin Neil Baily, an economist who is leading a research project on retirement security, notes that quitting a career can lead to feelings of isolation and loneliness, particularly for men.

译文：正主持退休保障研究项目的经济学家马丁·尼尔·贝利指出，离开职业生涯会带来孤立与孤独感，对男性尤其如此。

结构拆解：

· 主语 Martin Neil Baily + 同位语 an economist who is leading…，人名后同位语含定语从句。

· notes + that 宾语从句。

· 从句主语 quitting a career 为动名词短语；particularly for men 作补充说明。

翻译技巧：人名＋同位语（含 who 从句）先译成“……的经济学家”，再译主句。

2025年12月第2套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. Advertising campaigns from the snack food companies, often featuring sports stars, send the message that we can neutralize any negative effects of consuming their products simply by getting more physical exercise.

译文：零食公司的广告常由体育明星代言，它们传递这样的信息：只要多运动，就能抵消食用其产品的负面后果。

结构拆解：

· 主句：Advertising campaigns… send the message。

· often featuring sports stars 是现在分词作插入说明（常以体育明星为主角）。

· that 引导同位语从句说明 message 内容；by getting more physical exercise 作方式状语。

翻译技巧：send the message that… 译“传递这样的信息：……”，that 从句可加冒号。

2. The researchers argued that previous interventions have probably been unsuccessful because of a major flaw: they focused on a future, healthier you and assumed that this would be enough motivation for teenagers.

译文：研究者认为，此前干预之所以失败，很可能是因为一个重大缺陷：它们着眼于“未来更健康的你”，并以为这足以激励青少年。

结构拆解：

· argued + that 宾语从句。

· because of a major flaw + 冒号，冒号后解释 flaw 内容。

· they focused on… and assumed that… 两个并列谓语；a future, healthier you 用形容词临时名词化。

翻译技巧：冒号即解释，译文可加“也就是/即”；长句在 and 处断。

3. Apparently associating a healthy diet with teenagers’ own values seems to be a promising avenue to prevent obesity.

译文：显然，把健康饮食与青少年自身的价值观联系起来，似乎是预防肥胖颇有前景的途径。

结构拆解：

· 动名词短语 Associating a healthy diet with teenagers’ own values 作主语。

· seems to be 系表结构。

· to prevent obesity 不定式作定语修饰 avenue。

翻译技巧：动名词长主语先整句译完再出现谓语，避免头重脚轻。

4. When roused during REM sleep, 25% of the kids in Foulkes’ studies had no recollection of dreaming, a trend that continued with age.

译文：在快速眼动睡眠中被唤醒时，福克斯研究中 25% 的孩子对做过梦毫无记忆——这一趋势随年龄增长而延续。

结构拆解：

· When roused… 是省略的分词状语，还原为 When they were roused。

· 主句 25% of the kids… had no recollection of dreaming。

· a trend that continued with age 为总结性同位语：对前句事实作补充说明。

翻译技巧：句末“a/an + 名词 + that”多为总结性同位语，译“……，这一趋势……”。

2025年12月第3套 · 长难句精讲

3 句

1. With genetic testing becoming increasingly popular, many people are left wondering exactly how accurate it is.

译文：随着基因检测日益普及，许多人不禁疑惑：它到底有多准确？

结构拆解：

· With genetic testing becoming increasingly popular 是 with+宾语+现在分词的独立结构，表伴随/原因。

· be left wondering 意为“（被）留下悬念/陷入思考”。

· how accurate it is 为宾语从句。

翻译技巧：with+n+doing 译“随着/由于……”；be left wondering 译“不禁想知道”。

2. Whether you are taking a DNA test to build your extended DNA family tree, or want precise information on inborn health conditions, it is important to understand how accurate genetic tests are, and what information we can rely upon.

译文：无论你做 DNA 检测是为了构建扩展家谱，还是想获取先天健康状况的准确信息，弄清检测的准确性、以及哪些信息可以信赖，都很重要。

结构拆解：

· Whether… or… 引导让步状语从句（无论……还是……）。

· it 形式主语，真正主语是不定式 to understand how… and what…，内含两个并列的宾语从句。

翻译技巧：it is important to do… 译“……很重要”；两个宾语从句 how/ what 分开翻译。

3. Height, for example, depends on hundreds of genes, each contributing a little to the outcome, together with a bunch of environmental factors.

译文：以身高为例，它取决于数百个基因——每个基因都贡献一点点——外加一大堆环境因素。

结构拆解：

· 主句：Height depends on hundreds of genes。

· each contributing a little to the outcome 是“代词+现在分词”的独立主格，起补充说明作用。

· together with a bunch of environmental factors 为并列补充。

翻译技巧：独立主格 each contributing… 用破折号“而每个都……”处理，避免长句。

2025年6月第1套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. The experts concluded that pandas should ideally “be happy enough to thrive, but not so content that they don’t want to move around and find new mates”.

译文：专家们总结道：理想状态下熊猫应“足够幸福得以繁衍，但又不至于满足到不愿四处活动去寻找新伴侣”。

结构拆解：

· concluded + that 宾语从句。

· so… that… 表结果（太满足以致不想……）。

· happy enough to thrive 与 not so content that… 构成“够但不过”的对照。

翻译技巧：so content that 译“……到（不愿）……的地步”；把引语按原文结构保留。

2. The ideal level of perfectly livable habitat was found to be only 80% of an area, with the remainder either too harsh or too affected by human activity.

译文：研究发现，完全宜居栖息地的理想比例只占区域的 80%，其余部分要么过于严酷，要么深受人类活动影响。

结构拆解：

· 主句：The ideal level… was found to be…（被动+不定式）。

· with the remainder either too harsh or too affected 是 with+名词+并列补语的独立结构，表伴随/结果。

翻译技巧：with+名词+补语译“……，而其余部分……”；either…or… 译“要么……要么……”。

3. It may well be that the messy nature of their relationship with human efforts induces more animals to scatter or travel further, and might result in greater genetic connectivity and enhanced population persistence.

译文：很可能是它们与人类活动之间这种杂乱的关系，促使更多动物散开或走得更远，从而带来更大的基因连通性与更强的种群延续能力。

结构拆解：

· It may well be that… 意为“很可能……”，为固定句式。

· 主语 the messy nature… induces more animals to scatter or travel further，and might result in… 两个并列谓语。

· greater genetic connectivity / enhanced population persistence 为并列宾语。

翻译技巧：It may well be that… 直接译“很可能……”；and 前断开补“从而”。

4. When we think about attaining success — whether it’s landing that job or learning that new skill — our thoughts are immediately burdened by all the things we must first learn.

译文：一想到取得成功——无论是得到那份工作还是学会那项新技能——我们的脑海立刻被“必须先去学的一切”所压垮。

结构拆解：

· When 时间状语从句。

· 破折号内 whether… or… 为让步插入语。

· 主句是被动语态 our thoughts are burdened by…；we must first learn 为定语从句修饰 the things。

翻译技巧：破折号插入语可先跳过，译完主句再补“无论……还是……”。

5. Even if you haven’t mastered a skill, grit tells you that you can still succeed if you can transform your passion into action.

译文：即使你尚未精通某项技能，毅力也会告诉你：只要你能把热情转化为行动，你仍能成功。

结构拆解：

· Even if 引导让步状语从句。

· grit tells you that… 宾语从句。

· 宾语从句内部又含 if 条件状语从句。

翻译技巧：多层从句层层剥：先 Even if，再 that，最后内层 if，按“即使…只要…仍…”组织。

2025年6月第3套 · 长难句精讲

6 句

1. Analysts don’t expect the meat-free movement to slow anytime soon; a 2021 report predicts the market will skyrocket to $74 billion by 2030 — a 957 percent surge.

译文：分析师预计无肉食品风潮不会很快降温：2021 年的一份报告预测，到 2030 年市场规模将飙升至 740 亿美元——增幅达 957%。

结构拆解：

· 分号连接两个并列分句，结构是「主句①；主句②」。

· ②中 a 2021 report predicts + 宾语从句（省略 that），破折号后的 a 957 percent surge 是名词短语，作前面“增长到 740 亿”的同位补充。

· 语法点：expect + 宾语 + to do；predict 后接 that 从句；同位语补充说明。

翻译技巧：先按分号切成两个意群；破折号后的数字短语照译即可，不必硬翻成句子。

2. Companies such as Impossible Foods and Beyond Meat — the current superstars of the alternative protein sector — are pursuing consumers who enjoy meat but want to reduce their meat consumption for animal rights, health, or environmental reasons.

译文：像 Impossible Foods 和 Beyond Meat 这样的公司——植物基蛋白界的当红明星——正瞄准那些既爱吃肉、又想出于动物权利、健康或环保等原因少吃肉的消费者。

结构拆解：

· 主句主干：Companies… are pursuing consumers（公司正在争取消费者）。

· such as… 列举；“— the current superstars… —”双破折号是插入的同位语，解释两家公司，读时可先跳过。

· who enjoy meat but want to reduce… 是定语从句修饰 consumers，从句内 but 连接两个并列谓语；for…reasons 为原因状语。

翻译技巧：先抓主干“公司争取消费者”，再看 who 从句补全“什么样的消费者”；插入语最后处理。

3. “Maybe the best sign that Impossible Foods has cracked the code to realistic fake meat is that I couldn’t stand to take another bite,” she wrote.

译文：她写道：“也许 Impossible Foods 破解仿真肉密码的最好证据，就是我（被仿真肉像到）一口都吃不下去了。”

结构拆解：

· 主系表结构：the best sign is that…（最好的证明是……）。

· 第一个 that 引导同位语从句，说明 sign 的内容；第二个 that 引导表语从句。

· crack the code“破解密码/找到窍门”；couldn’t stand to take another bite 以“吃不下去”反证仿真度之高。

翻译技巧：两个 that 从句各管各的：前面同位语解释 sign，后面表语说明 is。引语内容整体提前，she wrote 后置。

4. Just read about the latest Hollywood starlets whose seeming love turns into resentment and bitter court battles.

译文：只需看看最新的好莱坞小明星——她们看似美好的爱情变成怨恨和难堪的官司。

结构拆解：

· 祈使句（无主语的命令/建议）：Just read about the latest Hollywood starlets。

· whose seeming love… 是定语从句修饰 starlets，whose 表“她们的”。

· seeming love“表面上的爱情”；turns into 后接两个并列宾语 resentment 与 bitter court battles。

翻译技巧：名词 + whose 从句 =“……的人/事，其……”，顺译即可；可加破折号或逗号断句。

5. While our initial attraction may be based, in part, on physical chemistry, it is the meeting of our inner worlds that creates the lasting intimacy and spiritual connection for which we long.

译文：尽管最初的吸引也许部分源于生理层面的化学反应，但真正带来我们渴望的持久亲密与精神联结的，是彼此内心世界的相遇。

结构拆解：

· While 引导让步状语从句“尽管……”。

· 主句是强调句：it is the meeting of our inner worlds that creates…，还原后为 The meeting of our inner worlds creates…（内心相遇创造了……）。

· for which we long 是定语从句修饰 connection（long for“渴望”，介词 for 前置到 which 前）。

翻译技巧：先译 While 让步分句；强调句译成“正是……才……”；for which 还原为 which we long for 再顺译。

6. Many of us spend far too much time, energy, and money trying to polish an image of what we think will bring attention, love, and connection.

译文：我们许多人花费太多的时间、精力和金钱去打磨一个自认为能带来关注、爱与联结的形象。

结构拆解：

· 主句主干：Many of us spend … time, energy, and money trying to polish an image。

· spend + 时间/金钱 + (in) doing 结构；trying to polish… 作方式/结果状语。

· what we think will bring… 是宾语从句（what 同时是从句主语），we think 是插入语，实为 what will bring attention, love, and connection。

翻译技巧：把 what we think 按插入语处理：“自认为会带来……的（形象）”。

2024年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. It shouldn’t be just a one-day event but a course integrated throughout the whole of high school that would allow students to gradually expand their financial literacy, and would prevent the need for a ‘catch up’ phase once we’re out on our own after graduation.

译文：财商课不应只是一天的事件，而应是贯穿整个高中的课程：它让学生逐步提升金融素养，也免去我们毕业后独自生活时“再追赶”的必要。

结构拆解：

· not… but… 结构，强调应为“课程”而非“一日活动”。

· integrated throughout… 为过去分词短语作定语修饰 course。

· that would allow… and would prevent… 定语从句内并列谓语；once 引导时间状语从句。

翻译技巧：not…but… 译“不是……而是……”；that 长定语从句可断开另起一句。

2. Giving young people the opportunity to become familiar with the world of money would provide them with a great advantage to enter adulthood with confidence and security so that they are able to manage their own money and look after themselves.

译文：给年轻人熟悉金钱世界的机会，能让他们带着信心与安全感步入成年时占据巨大优势，从而能够打理自己的钱并照顾好自己。

结构拆解：

· 动名词短语 Giving young people the opportunity… 作主语。

· provide them with a great advantage 双宾结构。

· so that 引导目的/结果状语从句。

翻译技巧：先译动名词主语，再按 provide…with… 与 so that 顺译。

3. Scientists believe that both of these countries will experience a 3.8 ℉ temperature increase by 2050 due to global warming, endangering the cacao farms in the rainforests.

译文：科学家认为，由于全球变暖，到 2050 年这两个国家的气温都将升高 3.8 华氏度，从而危及雨林中的可可种植园。

结构拆解：

· believe + that 宾语从句。

· due to global warming 表原因。

· 现在分词 endangering… 作结果状语，说明前面的后果。

翻译技巧：分词结尾作结果状语译“从而/以致……”；原因短语前移。

4. According to a report by The Business Insider, in the University of California’s new bio-sciences building, tiny green cacao seedlings are lined up in refrigerated greenhouses for a new experiment by using a technology called CRISPR.

译文：据《商业内幕》报道，在加州大学的新生命科学大楼里，绿色可可幼苗正被整齐排列在冷藏温室中，用于一项利用 CRISPR 技术的新实验。

结构拆解：

· According to… 与地点状语前置。

· 主句：seedlings are lined up in refrigerated greenhouses。

· for a new experiment by using a technology called CRISPR：目的＋方式；called CRISPR 为过去分词定语。

翻译技巧：多个状语按中文习惯“来源→地点→动作”排序翻译。

2024年12月第2套 · 长难句精讲

3 句

1. A tipping point was reached in 2020 when human-made materials — such as steel, concrete and plastic — were found to weigh more than all life on Earth.

译文：2020 年达到了一个临界点：钢铁、混凝土和塑料等人造材料的重量，被发现超过了地球上所有生命的重量。

结构拆解：

· 主句：A tipping point was reached in 2020。

· when 引导定语从句修饰 2020（=at which）。

· 破折号内 such as 举例；were found to weigh… 为被动+不定式。

翻译技巧：when 修饰年份时作定语从句译“……的那一年”，破折号举例后置。

2. Apart from the urgent task of providing more healthy nutrients to those who increasingly can’t afford them, publicly accessible fruit and vegetable gardens connect what we eat to where it comes from — the means of production, if you will.

译文：除了向越来越买不起营养食物的群体提供健康营养这一紧迫任务外，公共蔬果园还把我们“吃什么”与“它来自哪里”联系起来——可以说是生产方式本身。

结构拆解：

· Apart from… 介词短语作状语，内含 of providing… to those who… 结构。

· 主句：gardens connect what we eat to where it comes from，what/where 引导宾语从句作 connect 的两个宾语。

· 破折号后的 the means of production 为同位补充；if you will 意为“如果你愿意这么说”。

翻译技巧：connect A to B 要抓住 A、B 两个对比对象；同位语补充可另起短句。

3. One survey taker explained, “The greatest challenge for me was continuing to believe in myself, when all the messages I was getting were that I would never be taken seriously or promoted or given raises at the same rate as men, who were clearly less qualified and not as smart as I was.”

译文：一位受访者解释：“我最大的挑战是始终相信自己，尽管我得到的种种信息都在说：我永远不会像男性那样被认真对待、晋升或加薪——而那些男性显然没有我有资格、也没我聪明。”

结构拆解：

· 主句 The greatest challenge was continuing to believe in myself，动名词作表语。

· when 在此表让步，相当于 even though，译“尽管/虽然”。

· the messages I was getting were that…：定语从句＋表语从句；be taken seriously/promoted/given raises 为并列被动。

· who 定语从句修饰 men，内含同级比较 not as… as…。

翻译技巧：注意 when 的让步义；被动三连译“被认真对待/晋升/加薪”；who 从句最后补“而那些人……”。

2023年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. In other words, the more we believe that one has the opportunity for success based on hard work, the more we tend to think that people who skip leisure and work all the time are of higher standing.

译文：换言之，我们越相信“靠努力就能获得成功的机会”，就越倾向于认为：那些放弃休闲、一直工作的人地位更高。

结构拆解：

· the more…, the more… 越……越……。

· believe + that 宾语从句；based on hard work 分词短语作定语修饰 opportunity。

· think + that 宾语从句；who skip leisure and work all the time 定语从句修饰 people；be of higher standing＝be higher in status。

翻译技巧：the more…the more 两边各译“越……”；从句套从句逐层译。

2. That’s why we feel like we have to appear busy, and there’s a real perception that if someone is knee-deep in meetings, emails and stress, then they’re probably a big deal.

译文：这就是为什么我们总觉得必须显得很忙——也确实存在这样一种看法：一个人若深陷会议、邮件与压力之中，那大概是个“人物”。

结构拆解：

· That’s why… 为表语从句（这就是……的原因）。

· 并列 there’s a perception that…：that 引导同位语从句说明 perception 内容。

· 同位语从句内 if…then… 为条件句。

翻译技巧：that’s why 先定调，then 处译“那么/那就”；knee-deep 是口语“深陷”义。

3. We found that only the concept of “negative reciprocity” — in which an employee who feels unfairly treated engages in negative behaviors, such as spending their company’s money less carefully — explains these differences.

译文：我们发现，只有“负互惠”这个概念能解释这些差异——它指的是：感到自己受到不公对待的员工会做出消极行为，比如不那么谨慎地花公司的钱。

结构拆解：

· found + that 宾语从句，主干 only the concept… explains these differences。

· 双破折号内 in which… 定语从句修饰 concept，其中又含 who 定语从句修饰 employee。

· such as spending… 举例说明 negative behaviors。

翻译技巧：双破折号长定语最后处理：先译主句“只有……能解释”，再把 in which 从句作为解释补充。

4. Our analysis suggests early booking by women can translate into savings of $1 million a year for a large multinational company with 20,000 regular travelers.

译文：我们的分析表明，女性提前订票能为一家拥有 2 万名常旅客的大型跨国企业每年节省 100 万美元。

结构拆解：

· suggests + 宾语从句（省略 that）。

· early booking by women 为从句主语。

· translate into 意为“转化为/带来”；with 20,000 regular travelers 介词短语修饰 company。

翻译技巧：translate into 不要直译“翻译”，按语境译为“转化为/带来节省”。
