长难句精讲册（四步拆句法 · 全 10 套真题）

四步法：① 找主干（几个谓语切几段，先划掉从句/插入语）→ ② 理枝叶（每块修饰谁）→ ③ 排语序（定语前置/长状语拆句）→ ④ 出译文。标记：★＝嵌套较深可拆 2 句顺译；◎＝含插入语(—…—/(…))，先划掉再看主干。

四步拆句法 · 使用说明

口诀：一个句子有几个独立谓语动词，就切成几段；先把从句/介词短语/插入语（两个逗号或破折号之间）划掉，剩下的主谓宾/主系表就是主干。

步骤做什么
① 找主干找谓语→往前是主语，往后是宾语/表语
② 理枝叶每块是定语(修饰名词)还是状语(修饰动作/整句)？插语先放一边
③ 排语序英语右分支：定语/原因状语等往前提；太长或并列就拆两句顺译
④ 出译文按“主语→状语/定语→谓语→状语”串成通顺中文

例：The theory (which was proposed by Einstein) has changed our understanding (of the universe) (in the past century). → ①主干 The theory has changed our understanding. → ② which 定语修饰 theory；of 修饰 understanding；in 为时间状语 → ③ 前置 → ④ “在过去一个世纪里，爱因斯坦提出的那个理论，极大改变了我们对宇宙的理解。”

例外（可不前置）：并列长句(and/but)顺译；so…that…/with 引导的长结果或伴随状语太长时拆成短句顺译。

★ = 本句嵌套较深（约 3 层以上），建议先按①②③找到骨架，再拆成两句顺译，不必硬调语序。

2026年6月第2套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. Those people live in constant fear that their “deception” will be exposed.

① 主干Those people live in fear.（这些人活在恐惧中。）
② 枝叶that… will be exposed → 同位语从句，说明 fear 内容。
③ 语序fear that… 顺译：“恐惧——（怕）欺骗暴露”。
④ 译文这些人时刻担心：自己的“欺骗”迟早会被揭穿。

2. They think that all of their successes are not a product of their skill or hard work, instead they attribute their own successes to external circumstances, for example, to luck and chance, or believe that their performance is massively overestimated by others.

① 主干They think… instead they attribute… or believe…（他们认为…反而…或相信…）。
② 枝叶that 宾语从句×2（认为/相信）；instead 表“反而”；for example 为插入。
③ 语序三个动词 think→attribute→believe 平列，顺着译；“instead”译为“反而”。
④ 译文他们认为成功并非能力或努力的结果，反而归因于外部环境（如运气），或相信自己的表现被他人严重高估。

3. A permanent underestimation of one’s own abilities is often accompanied by the fear that this supposed intellectual deception will be exposed sooner or later and that they will pay the price for this.

① 主干Underestimation is accompanied by the fear.（低估伴随着恐惧。）
② 枝叶that… and that… → 两个并列同位语从句，都说明 fear。
③ 语序同位语从句按“恐惧——（怕）……”顺译。
④ 译文长期低估自己的能力，常伴随恐惧：怕这种“智力欺骗”迟早暴露，并为此付出代价。

4. It is astonishing how many cultures share this tool in approaching music.

① 主干It is astonishing…（令人惊讶）。
② 枝叶how many cultures share… → 主语从句（真正主语）；in approaching music → 状语。
③ 语序形式主语 it 先不译，主语从句放谓语后：“……令人惊讶”。
④ 译文令人惊讶的是，有这么多文化在接触音乐时都使用同一工具。

5. Using this five-note scale, musicians from around the globe have a medium through which they can relate, communicate, perform, and ultimately understand each other.

① 主干Musicians have a medium.（音乐家有了一种媒介。）
② 枝叶Using… → 方式状语；through which… → 定语从句，修饰 medium。
③ 语序Using… 状语提前译“借助……”；through which 还原“通过这个媒介”。
④ 译文借助这种五音音阶，全球的音乐家有了一个能沟通、交流、演奏并最终理解彼此的媒介。

2026年6月第3套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. Children experience physical disgust from a very early age, but do not achieve an understanding of the concept until they are around four years old.

① 主干Children experience disgust, but do not achieve understanding.（儿童有厌恶感，却未理解概念。）
② 枝叶not…until… → 时间状语（直到……才）；but 连接并列。
③ 语序not until 提前：“直到……才……”顺译。
④ 译文儿童很早就体验到生理性厌恶，但直到四岁左右才能真正理解这一概念。

2. Some say that when we refer to disgust in relation to moral issues, we are using disgust as a metaphor, and what we are feeling is in reality anger.

① 主干Some say that we are using disgust… and what we are feeling is anger.（有人说我们是用“厌恶”打比方，真实感受是愤怒。）
② 枝叶when we refer… → 时间状语从句；what we are feeling → 主语从句。
③ 语序when 状语先译；and 后分句顺译。
④ 译文有人说，当我们把“厌恶”用于道德问题时只是打比方——我们真实的感受其实是愤怒。

3. Indeed, children whose parents talk about emotions often have a better emotional understanding than children whose parents do not discuss emotions often.

① 主干Children have a better understanding than children.（A 的理解比 B 好。）
② 枝叶whose parents talk… / whose parents do not discuss… → 定语从句，分别修饰前后两个 children。
③ 语序把两个 whose“父母如何→孩子如何”前置，再套 better…than。
④ 译文父母常和孩子谈论情绪的孩子，情绪理解力好于父母很少谈论情绪的孩子。

4. Researchers Anuj Shah and Michael LaForest wondered whether our tendency to believe that social ties are reciprocal could lead us to mistakenly feel that a stranger does actually know us. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干Researchers wondered whether… tendency could lead us to feel…（研究者想知道这种倾向会不会让我们以为……）。
② 枝叶whether… → 宾语从句；to believe that… → 修饰 tendency；that a stranger knows us → 结果，作 feel 的宾语。
③ 语序先抓链条：倾向→导致→误以为；长句按链条分段译。
④ 译文研究者想知道：我们“关系是相互的”这种倾向，会不会让我们误以为陌生人其实认识我们。

5. The team found that residents of the developments that had received the leaflets believed it more likely that local officers would find out about illegal activity than residents of the control areas. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干Residents believed it more likely.（住户认为更有可能。）
② 枝叶that had received… → 定语，修饰 developments；it more likely that… → 形式宾语结构（真宾语是 that 从句）；than residents… → 比较对象。
③ 语序believed it likely that… 拆开译“更相信……”；than 提前作“比对照区住户”。
④ 译文团队发现，收到传单社区的住户比对照区住户更相信：警察会发现违法行为。

2025年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. Because how the body responds to a drug can differ depending on the time it is taken, it logically follows that taking medicines at specific times could help increase their effectiveness and reduce unwanted side effects.

① 主干… it follows that taking medicines could help increase effectiveness and reduce side effects.（于是，按时服药有助增效减副作用。）
② 枝叶Because how the body responds… → 原因状语，内含 how 主语从句；it is taken → 定语，修饰 time。
③ 语序Because 从句先译；两个 it 都是形式主语，分别还原成 how…/taking…。
④ 译文由于身体对药物的反应会随服药时间而不同，因此在特定时间服药能提高药效、减少副作用。

2. Over 50 years ago, researchers found that the cholesterol drug simvastatin is more effective at lowering cholesterol levels when taken at night rather than during the day.

① 主干Researchers found that the drug is more effective when taken at night.（研究者发现该药夜间服用更有效。）
② 枝叶when taken at night → 省略主语的分词状语（=when it is taken）；rather than… → 对比。
③ 语序when+过去分词还原“当……被服用时”→译成“夜间服用”。
④ 译文五十多年前研究者就发现，降胆固醇药辛伐他汀夜间服用比白天更有效。

3. In addition to people being healthier and living much longer, economists say that a combination of financial considerations such as years of slow rise in real wages and a shift away from traditional pensions in the private sector are some of the reasons people delay retirement.

① 主干Economists say that a combination… and a shift… are some of the reasons.（经济学家说，一串因素都是部分原因。）
② 枝叶In addition to people being healthier… → 状语（除……外）；such as… → 举例，修饰 considerations；people delay retirement → 定语，修饰 reasons。
③ 语序In addition to 先译；such as 长列举断开译成“诸如……等”。
④ 译文除人们更健康、活得更久外，经济学家说，实际工资缓涨、私营部门退出传统养老金等因素，也是人们推迟退休的部分原因。

4. Martin Neil Baily, an economist who is leading a research project on retirement security, notes that quitting a career can lead to feelings of isolation and loneliness, particularly for men.

① 主干Martin Neil Baily notes that quitting a career can lead to isolation.（贝利指出辞职会带来孤独。）
② 枝叶an economist who is leading… → 同位语（说明身份），内含 who 定语从句；that quitting… → 宾语从句。
③ 语序人名＋同位语先译成“……的经济学家”，再译 notes 内容。
④ 译文主持退休保障项目研究的经济学家贝利指出，辞职会带来孤立与孤独感，对男性尤甚。

2025年12月第2套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. Advertising campaigns from the snack food companies, often featuring sports stars, send the message that we can neutralize any negative effects of consuming their products simply by getting more physical exercise.

① 主干Advertising campaigns send the message.（广告传递信息。）
② 枝叶often featuring sports stars → 插入（分词补充说明）；that we can neutralize… → 同位语从句，说明 message；by getting… → 方式状语。
③ 语序插入语先跳过；message that… 用冒号译“信息：……”。
④ 译文零食公司的广告（常由体育明星代言）传递这样的信息：多运动就能抵消其产品的负面后果。

2. The researchers argued that previous interventions have probably been unsuccessful because of a major flaw: they focused on a future, healthier you and assumed that this would be enough motivation for teenagers. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干The researchers argued that interventions have been unsuccessful because of a flaw.（研究者认为干预失败系因一大缺陷。）
② 枝叶that… → 宾语从句；冒号后解释 flaw；they focused on… and assumed that… → 并列解释。
③ 语序冒号即解释，译文可加“也就是”；and 处断开。
④ 译文研究者认为，此前干预失败很可能缘于一大缺陷：它们着眼于“未来更健康的你”，并以为这足以激励青少年。

3. Apparently associating a healthy diet with teenagers’ own values seems to be a promising avenue to prevent obesity.

① 主干Associating a healthy diet with values seems to be a promising avenue.（把健康饮食与价值观挂钩似乎很有前景。）
② 枝叶Associating… → 动名词作主语；to prevent obesity → 定语，修饰 avenue。
③ 语序动名词长主语先整译，谓语后置。
④ 译文显然，把健康饮食与青少年的价值观联系起来，是预防肥胖颇有前景的途径。

4. When roused during REM sleep, 25% of the kids in Foulkes’ studies had no recollection of dreaming, a trend that continued with age.

① 主干25% of the kids had no recollection.（25% 的孩子毫无记忆。）
② 枝叶When roused… → 省略分词状语（=When they were roused）；a trend that… → 同位语+定语，总结前句。
③ 语序省略状语还原后置前；a trend that… 译“——这一趋势……”。
④ 译文在快速眼动睡眠中被唤醒时，25% 的孩子对做梦毫无记忆——这一趋势随年龄增长而延续。

2025年12月第3套 · 长难句精讲

3 句

1. With genetic testing becoming increasingly popular, many people are left wondering exactly how accurate it is.

① 主干Many people are left wondering how accurate it is.（许多人不禁想知道它有多准。）
② 枝叶With genetic testing becoming popular → 独立结构，表伴随/原因；how accurate it is → 宾语从句。
③ 语序with+n+doing 译“随着……”前置。
④ 译文随着基因检测日益普及，许多人不禁疑惑：它到底有多准确？

2. Whether you are taking a DNA test to build your extended DNA family tree, or want precise information on inborn health conditions, it is important to understand how accurate genetic tests are, and what information we can rely upon.

① 主干It is important to understand how accurate… and what…（弄清“多准、信什么”很重要。）
② 枝叶Whether… or… → 让步状语；it → 形式主语，真主语是 to understand how… and what…。
③ 语序Whether…or… 先译“无论……还是……”；it is important to… 译“……很重要”。
④ 译文无论你做 DNA 检测是为了扩展家谱还是了解先天健康信息，弄清检测多准、哪些信息可信都很重要。

3. Height, for example, depends on hundreds of genes, each contributing a little to the outcome, together with a bunch of environmental factors.

① 主干Height depends on hundreds of genes.（身高取决于数百个基因。）
② 枝叶each contributing a little… → 独立主格（补充说明）；together with… → 并列补充。
③ 语序独立主格用破折号“而每个都……”补充。
④ 译文以身高为例，它取决于数百个基因——每个贡献一点点——外加一堆环境因素。

2025年6月第1套 · 长难句精讲

5 句

1. The experts concluded that pandas should ideally “be happy enough to thrive, but not so content that they don’t want to move around and find new mates”.

① 主干The experts concluded that pandas should be happy enough to thrive, but not so content.（专家认为熊猫应“够快乐，但别太满足”。）
② 枝叶concluded that… → 宾语从句；so… that… → 结果状语（太满足以致……）。
③ 语序so content that 译“……到（不愿）……的地步”。
④ 译文专家总结：熊猫应“够快乐得以繁衍，但不至于满足到不愿去找新伴侣”。

2. The ideal level of perfectly livable habitat was found to be only 80% of an area, with the remainder either too harsh or too affected by human activity.

① 主干The ideal level was found to be 80%.（理想比例被发现为 80%。）
② 枝叶with the remainder either too harsh or too affected → 独立结构，表伴随结果。
③ 语序with+名词+补语译“……，而其余部分……”。
④ 译文研究发现，完全宜居栖息地的理想比例仅占区域的 80%——其余部分要么太严酷，要么深受人类活动影响。

3. It may well be that the messy nature of their relationship with human efforts induces more animals to scatter or travel further, and might result in greater genetic connectivity and enhanced population persistence.

① 主干The messy nature induces animals to scatter and might result in connectivity.（这种杂乱性促使动物散开并带来基因连通性。）
② 枝叶of their relationship with human efforts → 定语，修饰 nature；to scatter or travel further → 宾补；and might result in… → 并列谓语。
③ 语序It may well be that… 固定译“很可能……”；长定语前置。
④ 译文很可能正是它们与人类活动之间这种杂乱关系，促使更多动物散开或走远，从而带来更强的基因连通性与种群延续力。

4. When we think about attaining success — whether it’s landing that job or learning that new skill — our thoughts are immediately burdened by all the things we must first learn. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译 ◎有插入语·先划掉

① 主干Our thoughts are burdened by the things.（我们被各种“要学的”压垮。）
② 枝叶When we think… → 时间状语；破折号内 whether…or… → 让步插入；we must first learn → 定语，修饰 the things。
③ 语序插入语跳过；When 状语先译。
④ 译文一想到成功——无论得到那份工作还是学会新技能——我们就被“必须先学的种种”压垮。

5. Even if you haven’t mastered a skill, grit tells you that you can still succeed if you can transform your passion into action. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干Grit tells you that you can still succeed.（毅力告诉你：你仍能成功。）
② 枝叶Even if… → 让步状语；that you can succeed → 宾语从句，内含 if 条件状语。
③ 语序按“即使……只要……仍能……”组织三层。
④ 译文即使还没精通一项技能，毅力也会告诉你：只要把热情化为行动，你仍能成功。

2025年6月第3套 · 长难句精讲

6 句

1. Analysts don’t expect the meat-free movement to slow anytime soon; a 2021 report predicts the market will skyrocket to $74 billion by 2030 — a 957 percent surge.

① 主干Analysts don’t expect the movement to slow; a report predicts the market will skyrocket.（分析师不认为会降温；报告预测将飙升。）
② 枝叶分号连接两个并列分句；— a 957 percent surge → 同位语补充说明 $74 billion 的幅度。
③ 语序分号处断句；破折号数字短语照译。
④ 译文分析师预计无肉食品风潮不会很快降温；一份报告预测，到 2030 年市场规模将飙至 740 亿美元——增幅 957%。

2. Companies such as Impossible Foods and Beyond Meat — the current superstars of the alternative protein sector — are pursuing consumers who enjoy meat but want to reduce their meat consumption for animal rights, health, or environmental reasons. ◎有插入语·先划掉

① 主干Companies are pursuing consumers.（公司正争取消费者。）
② 枝叶— superstars… — → 插入同位语；who enjoy… but want to reduce… → 定语从句，修饰 consumers；for… reasons → 状语。
③ 语序插入语跳过；who 定语前置成“想吃肉又想少吃的消费者”。
④ 译文像 Impossible Foods、Beyond Meat 这类植物基蛋白明星公司，正争取那些爱吃肉却想为动物、健康或环保而少吃肉的消费者。

3. Maybe the best sign that Impossible Foods has cracked the code to realistic fake meat is that I couldn’t stand to take another bite.

① 主干The best sign is that I couldn’t stand to take another bite.（最好的证据是我一口都吃不下。）
② 枝叶第一个 that… → 同位语从句说明 sign；第二个 that… → 表语从句。
③ 语序两个 that 各管各的：同位语解释 sign，表语说明 is。
④ 译文也许 Impossible Foods 破解仿真肉密码的最好证据，就是我一口都吃不下去。

4. Just read about the latest Hollywood starlets whose seeming love turns into resentment and bitter court battles.

① 主干Read about the starlets.（去看看这些小明星。）
② 枝叶whose seeming love… → 定语从句，修饰 starlets。
③ 语序whose 从句译“……其……”顺译。
④ 译文看看那些好莱坞小明星吧——她们看似美好的爱情变成了怨恨和难堪的官司。

5. While our initial attraction may be based, in part, on physical chemistry, it is the meeting of our inner worlds that creates the lasting intimacy and spiritual connection for which we long. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干It is the meeting of our inner worlds that creates intimacy and connection.（是内心相遇创造了亲密与联结。）
② 枝叶While… → 让步状语；it is…that… → 强调句；for which we long → 定语从句，修饰 connection。
③ 语序While 先译“尽管”；强调句译“正是……才……”。
④ 译文尽管最初吸引也许部分来自生理层面，但正是内心世界的相遇创造了我们渴望的持久亲密与精神联结。

6. Many of us spend far too much time, energy, and money trying to polish an image of what we think will bring attention, love, and connection.

① 主干Many of us spend time, energy, and money trying to polish an image.（我们花大量心血去打磨一个形象。）
② 枝叶trying to polish… → 伴随/方式（spend … doing）；what we think will bring… → 宾语从句，of 的宾语；we think 为插入。
③ 语序把 what we think 按插入处理：“自认为会带来……的（形象）”。
④ 译文我们许多人花费太多时间、精力和金钱，去打磨一个自认为会带来关注、爱与联结的形象。

2024年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. It shouldn’t be just a one-day event but a course integrated throughout the whole of high school that would allow students to gradually expand their financial literacy, and would prevent the need for a ‘catch up’ phase once we’re out on our own after graduation.

① 主干It shouldn’t be an event but a course.（不应是活动，而应是课程。）
② 枝叶integrated… → 分词定语，修饰 course；that would allow… and would prevent… → 定语从句；once… → 时间状语。
③ 语序not…but… 译“不是……而是……”；that 长定语拆成另句。
④ 译文财商课不应只是一日活动，而应是一门贯穿整个高中的课程——它能让学生逐步提升金融素养，也免去毕业独自生活后再“追赶”。

2. Giving young people the opportunity to become familiar with the world of money would provide them with a great advantage to enter adulthood with confidence and security so that they are able to manage their own money and look after themselves.

① 主干Giving young people the opportunity would provide them with an advantage.（给年轻人机会=给他们优势。）
② 枝叶to become familiar… → 定语，修饰 opportunity；so that… → 目的/结果状语。
③ 语序动名词主语先译；so that 译“从而”。
④ 译文给年轻人熟悉金钱世界的机会，会让他们自信安全地步入成年并占据优势，从而能打理自己的钱、照顾好自己。

3. Scientists believe that both of these countries will experience a 3.8 ℉ temperature increase by 2050 due to global warming, endangering the cacao farms in the rainforests.

① 主干Scientists believe that both countries will experience a temperature increase.（科学家认为两国将升温。）
② 枝叶that… → 宾语从句；due to… → 原因状语；endangering… → 分词作结果状语。
③ 语序due to 提前译；endangering 译“从而危及”。
④ 译文科学家认为，由于全球变暖，到 2050 年这两国将升温 3.8°F，从而危及雨林中的可可种植园。

4. According to a report by The Business Insider, in the University of California’s new bio-sciences building, tiny green cacao seedlings are lined up in refrigerated greenhouses for a new experiment by using a technology called CRISPR.

① 主干Seedlings are lined up in greenhouses for an experiment.（幼苗被排列在温室里做实验。）
② 枝叶According to… → 状语（报道来源）；in the building → 地点状语；by using… → 方式状语；called CRISPR → 分词定语，修饰 technology。
③ 语序状语按“来源→地点”前置，再译主干。
④ 译文据《商业内幕》报道，在加州大学新生命科学大楼里，可可幼苗被排列在冷藏温室中，用 CRISPR 技术做新实验。

1. A tipping point was reached in 2020 when human-made materials — such as steel, concrete and plastic — were found to weigh more than all life on Earth. ◎有插入语·先划掉

① 主干A tipping point was reached in 2020.（2020 年到达临界点。）
② 枝叶when human-made materials… → 定语从句，修饰 2020；破折号 such as… → 插入举例。
③ 语序when 修饰年份译“……的那一年”，插入语后置。

2. Apart from the urgent task of providing more healthy nutrients to those who increasingly can’t afford them, publicly accessible fruit and vegetable gardens connect what we eat to where it comes from — the means of production, if you will. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干Gardens connect what we eat to where it comes from.（菜园把“吃什么”和“哪来的”连起来。）
② 枝叶Apart from… → 状语（除……外）；who increasingly can’t afford… → 定语，修饰 those；破折号 the means of production → 同位补充。
③ 语序Apart from 先译；connect A to B 抓两个宾语；同位语另起短句。
④ 译文除了给吃不起营养食物的群体供给健康营养外，公共蔬果园还把“吃什么”与“哪来的”连起来——即生产方式本身。

3. ① 主干The greatest challenge was continuing to believe in myself.（最大挑战是坚持相信自己。）
② 枝叶when all the messages were that… → 让步（尽管……）；I was getting → 定语修饰 messages；who → 定语修饰 men。
③ 语序when 表“尽管”先译；被动三连“被认真对待/晋升/加薪”；who 从句后补“而那些人……”。
④ 译文受访者说：“最大挑战是始终相信自己——尽管收到的信息都在说，我永远不会像男性那样被认真对待、晋升或加薪，而那些人显然没我有资格、没我聪明。”

2023年12月第1套 · 长难句精讲

4 句

1. In other words, the more we believe that one has the opportunity for success based on hard work, the more we tend to think that people who skip leisure and work all the time are of higher standing. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干The more we believe…, the more we tend to think…（越相信……就越认为……）。
② 枝叶that one has the opportunity… → 宾语从句；who skip leisure… → 定语，修饰 people。
③ 语序the more…the more 两边各译“越……”；定语前置。
④ 译文换言之，我们越相信“努力就能获得成功机会”，就越认为放弃休闲、一直工作的人地位更高。

2. That’s why we feel like we have to appear busy, and there’s a real perception that if someone is knee-deep in meetings, emails and stress, then they’re probably a big deal. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干That’s why we feel… and there’s a perception.（这就是我们觉得……的原因，也确实有这种看法。）
② 枝叶why we feel… → 表语从句；that if… then… → 同位语从句，说明 perception。
③ 语序that’s why 定调；then 处译“那就”。
④ 译文这就是我们觉得必须显得忙的原因——也确实有种看法：若有人深陷会议、邮件与压力中，那大概是个“人物”。

3. We found that only the concept of “negative reciprocity” — in which an employee who feels unfairly treated engages in negative behaviors, such as spending their company’s money less carefully — explains these differences. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译 ◎有插入语·先划掉

① 主干Only the concept explains these differences.（只有“负互惠”能解释这些差异。）
② 枝叶in which… → 定语从句修饰 concept，内嵌 who 定语从句修饰 employee；such as spending… → 举例。
③ 语序双破折号长定语后处理，先译主句再补解释。
④ 译文我们发现，只有“负互惠”这个概念能解释这些差异——即受不公对待的员工会做出消极行为，比如不那么谨慎地花公司钱。

4. Our analysis suggests early booking by women can translate into savings of $1 million a year for a large multinational company with 20,000 regular travelers.

① 主干Our analysis suggests early booking can translate into savings.（分析表明：早订=省钱。）
② 枝叶suggests + 宾语从句（省 that）；with 20,000 regular travelers → 定语，修饰 company。
③ 语序translate into 译“转化为/带来”；with 定语前置。
④ 译文我们的分析表明，女性早订票能为一家有 2 万名常旅客的大型跨国企业每年节省 100 万美元。
