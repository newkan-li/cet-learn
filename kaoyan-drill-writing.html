2020 年 · 写作（Part A 小作文 / Part B 大作文）

Part A 小作文

The student union of your university has assigned you to inform the international students about an upcoming singing contest. Write a notice in about 100 words.

参考范文

Notice

A singing contest will be held by the Student Union at 7 p.m. on May 20 in the university hall.

All international students are welcome to take part. Each contestant may sing one song, in Chinese or in your own language.

Please sign up at the Student Union office before May 15, or send your name and the song title to singing@univ.edu.

Come and show your talent! We look forward to your participation.

The Student Union

Part B 大作文

Write an essay of 160–200 words based on the pictures below (theme: 习惯/habit). 1) describe the pictures; 2) interpret the implied meaning; 3) give your comments.

图内容含义
图1一个人坚持每天读书/锻炼好习惯成就人生
图2一个人沉迷手机、拖延坏习惯消耗时间

提纲：P1 描述图画；P2 解读含义；P3 评论建议

参考范文

As is shown in the pictures, two people lead very different lives. One keeps reading and exercising every day, while the other is always glued to his phone and puts off what he should do.

The pictures tell us that habits shape our life. Good habits, such as reading and taking exercise, help us gain knowledge and keep healthy, while bad habits like staying up late and delaying tasks waste our time and energy.

In my view, we should attach great importance to the power of habits. We can start with small things and keep them every day. Little by little, good habits will lead us to a better and more meaningful life.

2021 年 · 写作（Part A 小作文 / Part B 大作文）

Part A 小作文

A foreign friend of yours has recently graduated from college and intends to find a job in China. Write him/her an email to make some suggestions.

参考范文

Dear Tom,

I am glad to hear that you have graduated and plan to find a job in China. Here are some suggestions for you.

First, you may learn some basic Chinese, which will help you communicate with colleagues and clients. Second, make good use of job websites and campus career centers to find positions that suit your major. Third, prepare a clear resume in both English and Chinese, and get ready for interviews.

I hope these tips are helpful. I am looking forward to seeing you in China.

Yours sincerely,

Li Ming

Part B 大作文

Write an essay of 160–200 words based on the picture below. 1) describe the picture; 2) interpret the implied meaning; 3) give your comments. 图中孩子说：“爸爸，很多同学觉得学唱戏不好玩。”父亲答：“你自己不是喜欢吗？那就足够了。”

图内容含义
图孩子对父亲说“同学觉得学唱戏不好玩”外界的看法/评价
图父亲答“你自己不是喜欢吗？那就足够了”尊重兴趣、坚持自我

提纲：P1 描述图画；P2 解读含义；P3 评论建议

参考范文

As is shown in the picture, a child tells his father that many of his classmates think learning to sing opera is not fun. The father answers calmly, “Don’t you like it yourself? That is enough.”

The picture conveys a meaningful message: when we pursue something we truly love, we should not be swayed by others’ opinions. Interest is the best teacher and the strongest motivation. If we give up our hobbies simply because others laugh at them, we may lose both our passion and our confidence. Besides, parents’ understanding and support are of great importance to children, for they can help the young keep their dreams alive.

In my view, we should learn to listen to our own hearts and persist in what we really enjoy. At the same time, parents and teachers should respect children’s interests and encourage them to develop their own talents. Only in this way can we live a fuller and happier life.

As is shown in the picture, villagers are gathering to watch a dragon boat race held in their own village, and an old man says with delight, “How wonderful! Our village’s dragon boat team…” The scene is full of joy and vitality.

The picture conveys a meaningful message: fine traditional culture is coming back to life in people’s daily lives. The dragon boat race is not merely a competition; it reflects the unity and hard work of the whole community and shows our growing cultural confidence. As traditional festivals and customs are revived, people feel a stronger sense of belonging and pride.

In my view, we should attach great importance to carrying forward our excellent traditional culture. Schools and communities can hold more activities such as dragon boat races and festival celebrations to let the young take part in them. Only when tradition is rooted in everyday life can it be passed down from generation to generation.

2024 年 · 写作（Part A 小作文 / Part B 大作文）

Part A 小作文

Read the following email from an international student and write a reply. Paul: I’ve got a class assignment to make an oral report on an ancient Chinese scientist, but I’m not sure how to prepare for it. Can you give me some advice?

参考范文

Dear Paul,

Glad to help. Here is some advice on preparing your oral report.

First, choose a scientist you find interesting, such as Zhang Heng or Li Shizhen, and read about his life and main achievements. Second, focus on one or two of his inventions and explain how they changed people’s lives. Third, make a simple outline and use pictures or a short video to make your report vivid. Finally, practise your speech several times so that you can speak clearly and confidently.

If you need more materials, just let me know. Good luck with your report.

Yours,

Li Ming

Part B 大作文

Write an essay based on the charts below. 1) describe the charts; 2) interpret the charts; 3) give your comments. 调查：消费者对养老机器人的接受度——完全接受 27.9%、部分接受 39.3%、不接受 32.8%；首要关注点——安全 46.30%、价格 24.90%、便利 10.70%。

类别占比
完全接受27.9%
部分接受39.3%
不接受32.8%
首要关注：安全46.30%
首要关注：价格24.90%
首要关注：便利10.70%

提纲：P1 描述图表；P2 解读含义；P3 评论建议

参考范文

As is shown in the charts, consumer attitudes towards elderly-care robots vary. About 27.9% of respondents fully accept them and 39.3% partly accept them, while 32.8% do not. As for their primary concerns, safety ranks first at 46.30%, followed by price at 24.90% and convenience at 10.70%.

The charts reveal that although most consumers welcome elderly-care robots, many still hesitate. Their biggest worry is safety, which is quite reasonable, since robots may work with the elderly when no family member is around. Price also matters, for such products should be affordable for ordinary families.

In my view, elderly-care robots are a promising solution to the growing need for care in an aging society. To win wider acceptance, companies should first ensure the safety and reliability of their products and keep prices reasonable. With the joint efforts of technology and regulation, robots will play a greater role in improving the lives of the elderly.
