Most scientists and experts sharply dispute Hardy’s views. Scientific papers are the recordkeepers of progress in research. Each year researchers publish millions of papers in more than 30,000 journals. The advent of electronic publishing and authors’ need to find outlets for their papers resulted in thousands of new journals. The birth of predatory journals wasn’t far behind. These journals can act as milk cows where every single article in an issue may cite a specific paper or a series of papers. There has been some exploration around the use of AI in digital marketing. For example, AI can be used to analyse what type of advertising content or copy would be appropriate to ‘speak’ to a specific target customer group by revealing information about trends and preferences through the analysis of big data. (46) AI can also be used to identify the lifestyle choices of customers regarding their hobbies, favourite celebrities, music choices, and fashions to provide unique content in marketing messages put out through social media. At the same time AI can also be used to generate content for social media posts and chat sites. AI can also provide a bridge between the need of the brand to communicate emotionally with the customer and identifying their rapidly changing needs. The main disadvantage of using AI to respond to customers is that there are concerns about trusting personal interactions to machines, which could lead not only to the subsequent loss of interpersonal connections, but also to a decrease in marketing personnel. (47) Some believe that AI is negatively impacting on the marketer’s role by reducing creativity and removing jobs, but they are aware that it is a way of reducing costs and creating new information. 长难句精讲（四步拆句 · 一目了然）

四步法：① 找主干（几个谓语切几段，先划掉从句/插入语）→ ② 理枝叶（每块修饰谁）→ ③ 排语序（定语前置/长状语拆句）→ ④ 出译文。

1. The weather in Texas may have cooled since the recent extreme heat, but the temperature will be high at the State Board of Education meeting in Austin this month as officials debate how climate change is taught in Texas schools.

① 主干The temperature will be high at the meeting.（会议上的“温度”将会很高。）
② 枝叶may have cooled 与 will be high 形成对比（双关）；but 转折；as officials debate… → 时间/原因状语从句；how… → 宾语从句。
③ 语序让步/对比顺译；as 从句译“随着/当……时”；temperature 双关译加引号。
④ 译文自最近的极端高温以来，得克萨斯州的天气或许已转凉，但本月在奥斯汀举行的州教育委员会会议上，“温度”将会很高，因为官员们将辩论该州学校如何教授气候变化。

2. Board members like her “casually dismiss the career work of scholars and scientists as just another misguided opinion,” says Dan Quinn.

① 主干Board members dismiss the career work as just another opinion.（委员们把学者们的毕生工作贬为又一种错误观点。）
② 枝叶like her → 介词短语作后置定语；dismiss A as B → 把 A 贬为 B；says Dan Quinn → 倒装引述。
③ 语序引述部分“丹·奎因说”可前置；dismiss…as 译“把……贬为……”。
④ 译文丹·奎因说，像她这样的委员“轻率地把学者和科学家的毕生工作贬为又一种错误的观点”。

3. A study last year by the National Center for Science Education, a non-profit group of scientists and teachers, looking at how state public schools across the country address climate change in science classes, gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.

① 主干A study gave barely half of US states a grade B+ or higher.（一项研究给不到一半的州打了 B+ 或更高。）
② 枝叶by the National Center… → 后置定语；a non-profit group… → 同位语；looking at how… → 现在分词作后置定语；address climate change 应对气候变化。
③ 语序同位语与分词定语用括号/“……的”译出；主干顺译。
④ 译文去年，由科学家和教师组成的非营利组织“国家科学教育中心”开展了一项研究，考察全国各州公立学校在科学课上如何处理气候变化问题，结果只有不到一半的州获得 B+ 或更高评分。

4. Even if a state is considered a high performer in its science standards, “that does not mean it will be taught”, he says.

① 主干That does not mean it will be taught.（那并不意味着它会被教授。）
② 枝叶Even if… → 让步状语从句；be considered a high performer 被认为表现出色；he says 引述。
③ 语序让步从句译“即使……”；引述“他说”可前置或后置。
④ 译文他说，即使某个州在科学标准上被认为表现出色，“那也并不意味着它会被真正教授”。

5. It comes at a time when authors are already worried about the consequences of the proposed merger between PRH and another big publisher, Simon & Schuster — the reduction in the number of unaligned UK publishers is likely to lead to fewer bidding wars, lower advances, and more conformity in terms of what is published.

① 主干It comes at a time when authors are worried about the consequences.（此事发生之际，作者们本就担忧其后果。）
② 枝叶when… → 定语从句修饰 a time；the proposed merger… → 同位语；破折号后为对后果的解释；what is published → 宾语从句。
③ 语序定语从句译“在……之际”；破折号后顺译解释“独立出版商减少将导致……”。
④ 译文此事发生之际，作者们本已担忧 PRH 与另一家大出版商西蒙与舒斯特拟议合并的后果——英国独立出版商数量的减少，可能导致竞价战减少、预付稿酬降低，以及出版内容更加趋同。

6. “This is all part of a wider change towards concentration of power,” says literary agent Andrew Lownie.

① 主干This is part of a wider change.（这是更大变革的一部分。）
② 枝叶towards concentration of power → 介词短语作后置定语；says literary agent… → 引述倒装。
③ 语序引述“文学经纪人安德鲁·朗尼说”可前置。
④ 译文文学经纪人安德鲁·朗尼说：“这都是朝向权力集中这一更大变革的一部分。”

7. Scopus itself has all the data necessary to detect this malpractice.

① 主干Scopus has all the data.（Scopus 拥有全部数据。）
② 枝叶itself 强调；necessary to detect… → 形容词短语作后置定语修饰 data。
③ 语序后置定语前置译“检测这种不当行为所需的（全部数据）”。
④ 译文Scopus 本身拥有检测这种不当行为所需的全部数据。

8. The non-intrusive delivery of the marketing messages in a way that is sensitive to the needs of the target customer is one of the critical challenges to the digital marketer.

① 主干The delivery is one of the critical challenges.（这种传递方式是重大挑战之一。）
② 枝叶of the marketing messages → 后置定语；in a way that… → 方式状语从句；be sensitive to 体察；one of the… → 表语。
③ 语序长主语顺译，方式状语提前“以……的方式”；one of… 译“……之一”。
④ 译文以敏锐体察目标客户需求的方式非侵入性地传递营销信息，是数字营销者面临的重大挑战之一。

语法专项（本卷句子 · 先做后对）

本套卷语法考点清单与教学

一、时态：一般现在 / 现在完成 / 一般过去

普遍规律与观点用一般现在（depend on / is considered）；由过去持续或强调影响用现在完成（have attempted / has dropped）；叙述历史事件用一般过去（captured / began / signed）。

二、被动语态

动作承受者作主语用 be+过去分词：were located / be subjected to / is considered / were given；注意 be considered + 名词/形容词。

三、非谓语动词

动名词作主语或宾语（preserving nature / removing jobs）；不定式作目的、定语或宾语（aimed to produce / necessary to detect / advised to add）；分词作定语或状语（looking at how… / influenced by…）。

四、三大从句

宾语从句：debate how climate change is taught / no-one is sure what the outcomes…；定语从句：a time when authors are worried / algorithms that are used…；让步状语从句：Even if a state is considered…；方式状语从句：in a way that is sensitive…。
