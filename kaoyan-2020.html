Text 1（“文化之城”奖）

A group of Labour MPs, among them Yvette Cooper, are bringing in the new year with a call to institute a UK “town of culture” award. The proposal is that it should sit alongside the existing city of culture title, which was held by Hull in 2017, and has been awarded to Coventry for 2021. Cooper and her colleagues argue that the success of the crown for Hull, where it brought in £220m of investment and an avalanche of arts, ought not to be confined to cities. Britain’s towns, it is true, are not prevented from applying, but they generally lack the resources to put together a bid to beat their bigger competitors. A town of culture award could, it is argued, become an annual event, attracting funding and creating jobs.

Some might see the proposal as a booby prize for the fact that Britain is no longer able to apply for the much more prestigious title of European capital of culture, a sought-after award bagged by Glasgow in 1990 and Liverpool in 2008. A cynic might speculate that the UK is on the verge of disappearing into an endless fever of self-celebration in its desperation to reinvent itself for the post-Brexit world: after town of culture, who knows what will follow — village of culture? Suburb of culture? Hamlet of culture?

It is also wise to recall that such titles are not a cure-all. A badly run “year of culture” washes in and out of a place like the tide, bringing prominence for a spell but leaving no lasting benefits to the community. The really successful holders of such titles are those that do a great deal more than fill hotel bedrooms and bring in high-profile arts events and good press for a year. They transform the aspirations of the people who live there; they nudge the self-image of the city into a bolder and more optimistic light. It is hard to get right, and requires a remarkable degree of vision, as well as cooperation between city authorities, the private sector, community groups and cultural organisations. But it can be done: Glasgow’s year as European capital of culture can certainly be seen as one of a complex series of factors that have turned the city into the powerhouse of art, music and theatre that it remains today.

A “town of culture” could be not just about the arts but about honouring a town’s peculiarities — helping sustain its high street, supporting local facilities and above all celebrating its people. Jeremy Wright, the culture secretary, should welcome this positive hope-filled proposal, and turn it into action.

Last Thursday, the French Senate passed a digital services tax, which would impose an entirely new tax on large multinationals that provide digital services to consumers or users in France. Digital services include everything from providing a platform for selling goods and services online to targeting advertising based on user data, and the tax applies to gross revenue from such services. Many French politicians and media outlets have referred to this as a “GAFA tax,” meaning that it is designed to apply primarily to companies such as Google, Apple, Facebook and Amazon — in other words, multinational tech companies based in the United States.

The digital services tax now awaits the signature of President Emmanuel Macron, who has expressed support for the measure, and it could go into effect within the next few weeks. At the same time, the European Union, Spain, Britain and several other countries have all seriously contemplated digital services taxes.

原题全文（划线句为 46–50，未省略）

Following the explosion of creativity in Florence during the 14th century known as the Renaissance, the modern world saw a departure from what it had once known. It turned from God and the authority of the Roman Catholic Church and instead favoured a more humanistic approach to being. Renaissance ideas had spread throughout Europe well into the 17th century, with the arts and sciences flourishing extraordinarily among those with a more logical disposition. (46) With the Church’s teachings and ways of thinking being eclipsed by the Renaissance, the gap between the Medieval and modern periods had been bridged, leading to new and unexplored intellectual territories. During the Renaissance, the great minds of Nicolaus Copernicus, Johannes Kepler and Galileo Galilei demonstrated the power of scientific study and discovery. (47) Before each of their revelations, many thinkers at the time had sustained more ancient ways of thinking, including the geocentric view that the Earth was at the centre of our universe. Copernicus theorized in 1543 that in actual fact, all of the planets that we knew of revolved not around the Earth, but the Sun, a system that was later upheld by Galileo at his own expense. (48) Despite attempts by the Church to suppress this new generation of logicians and rationalists, more explanations for how the universe functioned were being made at a rate that the people could no longer ignore. It was with these great revelations that a new kind of philosophy founded in reason was born. The Church’s long-standing dogma was losing the great battle for truth to rationalists and scientists. This very fact embodied the new ways of thinking that swept through Europe during most of the 17th century. The 17th and 18th centuries were times of radical change and curiosity. Scientific method, reductionism and the questioning of Church ideals was to be encouraged, as were ideas of liberty, tolerance and progress. (50) Such actions to seek knowledge and to understand what information we already knew were captured by the Latin phrase “sapere aude” or “dare to know”, after Immanuel Kant used it in his essay “An Answer to the Question: What is Enlightenment?”.

46. With the Church’s teachings and ways of thinking being eclipsed by the Renaissance, the gap between the Medieval and modern periods had been bridged, leading to new and unexplored intellectual territories.

答案

随着文艺复兴使教会的教义和思维方式黯然失色，中世纪与现代之间的鸿沟得以弥合，从而开启了崭新的、未被探索的知识领域。

答案

在他们各自作出这些揭示之前，当时的许多思想家一直固守着更古老的思维方式，包括认为地球位于宇宙中心的地心说。

随着许多人承担起把理性与科学哲学融入世界的责任，文艺复兴结束了，一个新的时代——理性时代——到来了。

长难句精讲（四步拆句 · 一目了然）

四步法：① 找主干（几个谓语切几段，先划掉从句/插入语）→ ② 理枝叶（每块修饰谁）→ ③ 排语序（定语前置/长状语拆句）→ ④ 出译文。

1. Even if families don’t sit down to eat together as frequently as before, millions of Britons will nonetheless have got a share this weekend of one of the nation’s great traditions: the Sunday roast.

① 主干Millions of Britons will have got a share of one of the nation’s great traditions.（数百万英国人将分得一份这一伟大传统。）
② 枝叶Even if… → 让步状语从句；as frequently as before → 比较状语；nonetheless 副词“仍然”；冒号后 the Sunday roast 解释 tradition。
③ 语序让步从句提前译“即使……”，主句顺译，冒号后补充说明。
④ 译文即使如今家庭聚餐不如以往频繁，本周末数百万英国人仍将分得一份这个国家的伟大传统——周日烤肉。

2. Yet as we report now, the food police are determined that this enjoyment should be rendered yet another guilty pleasure guaranteed to damage our health.

① 主干The food police are determined that this enjoyment should be rendered a guilty pleasure.（“食品警察”认定这份享受应被说成又一罪恶快感。）
② 枝叶as we report now → 时间/方式状语；be determined that… → 宾语从句；guaranteed to damage… → 过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 guilty pleasure。
③ 语序分词定语前置译“……的”；宾语从句顺译。
④ 译文然而正如我们此刻所报道的，“食品警察”认定这份享受应被说成又一个必定损害健康的罪恶快感。

3. But where is the evidence to support such alarmist advice? While studies have shown that acrylamide can cause neurological damage in mice, there is no conclusive evidence that it causes cancer in humans. ★ 嵌套较深：建议拆成 2 句顺译

① 主干Where is the evidence? / There is no conclusive evidence.（证据何在？没有确凿证据。）
② 枝叶to support… → 不定式作后置定语；While… → 让步状语从句；that it causes cancer in humans → 同位语从句，说明 evidence。
③ 语序同位语从句译“（证明）它会导致人类癌症的（证据）”；让步从句提前。
④ 译文但支持这种危言耸听建议的证据何在？尽管研究显示丙烯酰胺可致小鼠神经损伤，却没有确凿证据表明它会使人患癌。

4. The proposal is that it should sit alongside the existing city of culture title, which was held by Hull in 2017, and has been awarded to Coventry for 2021.

① 主干The proposal is that it should sit alongside the title.（该提议主张它与现有称号并列。）
② 枝叶that… → 表语从句；which was held by Hull… → 非限制性定语从句，修饰 title；并列谓语 has been awarded to Coventry。
③ 语序定语从句后置顺译，用“这一称号”衔接。
④ 译文该提议主张这一奖项应与现有的“文化之城”称号并列——该称号 2017 年由赫尔获得，并已被授予 2021 年的考文垂。

5. A cynic might speculate that the UK is on the verge of disappearing into an endless fever of self-celebration in its desperation to reinvent itself for the post-Brexit world.

① 主干A cynic might speculate that the UK is on the verge of disappearing.（怀疑者或许会推测英国正濒于消失。）
② 枝叶that… → 宾语从句；disappearing into… → 动名词短语；in its desperation to… → 介词短语表原因/状态，reinvent itself for…。
③ 语序宾语从句顺译，into an endless fever… 译“陷入无休止的自我庆贺狂热”。
④ 译文怀疑者或许会推测，英国为脱欧后的世界急于重塑自我，正濒于陷入无休止的自我庆贺狂热。

6. The really successful holders of such titles are those that do a great deal more than fill hotel bedrooms and bring in high-profile arts events and good press for a year.

① 主干The holders are those.（真正成功的称号拥有者是那样一些地方。）
② 枝叶that do a great deal more than… → 定语从句修饰 those；more than 后为并列动词 fill / bring in；high-profile 形容词作定语。
③ 语序定语从句译“（那些）所做的远不止……的地方”。
④ 译文真正成功的称号拥有者所做的，远不止一年里填满酒店客房、引来高知名度的艺术活动和好评。

7. In response to these many unilateral measures, the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) is currently working with 131 countries to reach a consensus by the end of 2020 on an international solution.

◎ 有插入语(—…— / (…))：第①步先划掉，主干立即清晰

① 主干The OECD is working with 131 countries to reach a consensus.（经合组织正与 131 国合作以达成共识。）
② 枝叶In response to… → 介词短语表原因；to reach a consensus → 不定式表目的；by the end of 2020 / on an international solution → 时间与对象状语。
③ 语序目的状语译“以……”；时间与对象状语前置“就国际方案在 2020 年底前”。
④ 译文针对这些众多的单边措施，经济合作与发展组织（OECD）目前正与 131 个国家合作，以求在 2020 年底前就一项国际方案达成共识。

8. Such actions to seek knowledge and to understand what information we already knew were captured by the Latin phrase “sapere aude” or “dare to know”, after Immanuel Kant used it in his essay.

① 主干Such actions were captured by the Latin phrase.（这类行动被这一拉丁语短语所概括。）
② 枝叶to seek knowledge and to understand… → 不定式作定语修饰 actions；what information we already knew → 宾语从句；after… → 时间状语从句。
③ 语序不定式定语译“探求知识、理解已知信息的（行动）”；after 从句后置顺译。
④ 译文这种探求知识、理解已知信息的行动，被拉丁语短语“sapere aude”（即“敢于求知”）所概括——伊曼努尔·康德曾在其文章中使用这一短语。

语法专项（本卷句子 · 先做后对）

本套卷语法考点清单与教学

一、时态：一般现在 / 现在完成 / 一般过去

客观事实与普遍规律用一般现在（few pleasures can match it / studies have shown）；由过去持续到现在或强调影响用现在完成（has been awarded / have shown / has been increasing）；叙述历史事件用一般过去（was held / passed / made）。

二、被动语态

动作承受者作主语用 be+过去分词：should be rendered / is designed to / were being made / is now published；注意“be+过去分词”表状态与表动作的区分。

三、非谓语动词

动名词作主语或宾语（reinventing itself / catching someone’s attention）；不定式作目的或后置定语（to seek knowledge / to reach a consensus）；分词作定语或状语（leading to… / guaranteed to damage…）。

四、三大从句

宾语从句：the proposal is that… / speculate that…；定语从句：that do a great deal more / which was held by Hull；让步状语从句：Even if… / While…；同位语从句：evidence that it causes cancer。
